## Verdict policy used

- **CORRECT:** every element that is printed matches the real record (authors, order, initials, year, title, venue, ID/DOI). Omitted elements (volume, pages, venue) are not errors; they are flagged "FYI". A year equal to the year of the version identified by the printed arXiv ID, URL, or venue counts as matching even if a later published version exists.
- **MINOR ERROR:** a printed element is wrong in a small way (initials, punctuation, page numbers), or a printed year is off by one against the printed venue/DOI because a preprint year was used.
- **MAJOR ERROR:** wrong authors or title, an ID/DOI resolving to a different work, or a venue that never published it.
- **NOT FOUND:** no trace after a reasonable search.
- A parenthetical on the verdict (for example "duplicate of 82") does not change the category.

## Notes

### Method and sources

- **arXiv:** one https call to `https://export.arxiv.org/api/query?id_list=2411.13537,2505.22954,2005.11401,2210.03629,2309.02427,2311.02462,2308.08708,2408.06292,2506.05109,2511.02824,2601.11974,2609.11873,2303.11366,2305.16291,2310.08560&max_results=50` returned 15 of 15 entries; titles and author lists were compared with the printed entries. `https://arxiv.org/abs/<id>` returned HTTP 200 for all 15, including the printed Vol I URLs.
- **DOIs:** Crossref `works/<doi>` for the 14 distinct printed DOIs (title, ordered authors, container, volume, issue, pages, issued date), plus extra lookups for the publisher DOIs of 57, 63, and 64 and the journal DOI of the MUSE paper (65). `https://doi.org/<doi>` returned 302 to the publisher landing page for all 14.
- **PubMed:** NCBI E-utilities esummary for PMIDs 21521609 and 27609244.
- **URL-only entries (57, 59, 63, 64):** each PDF was downloaded into its own empty directory and the first page was read with `pdftotext`; title and byline match.
- **Author/initials check:** the printed author strings were extracted programmatically from both lists (36 of 36 parsed, matching a hand transcription) and compared surname by surname and initial by initial against the registry names. One mismatch (57). All 36 printed years and all title strings were also compared programmatically; the only difference in titles is Pearl's Crossref short title "Causality" versus the book's full title with subtitle, which Crossref carries in its subtitle field.
- **Web search:** used for venue and publication status (Hahm et al., the 2026 papers, DGM, MemGPT/Letta, Russell 1995, MUSE, CoALA, Voyager, Morris et al., Liu and van der Schaar, Friston et al., MIDCA, Reflexion, RAG, ReAct, Hafner et al., LIDA, Generative Agents, Kaelbling et al., the AI Scientist, Kosmos, Butlin et al., Egeth and Kurzban). NeurIPS 2023 proceedings page fetched directly for the Reflexion author list.
- **Not circumvented:** dblp.org returned a bot challenge, so the Hahm et al. venue was taken from Crossref and Springer metadata instead. The Letta docs naming-history page is client-rendered and returned no usable text; the GitHub README was used.

### Body-grep method

- Body files were cut at the line before each reference list (`head -n 5542` for Vol I, `head -n 8360` for Vol II), so line numbers match the original `.txt` files. Searches used `rg -n` for the first author's surname (word-bounded for common words: Pearl, Howard, Williams, Lewis, Morris, Park, Hou, Lu, Liu, Yao, Zhang, Turner, Cox, Pine) and for the work's short name or title phrase (for example RAG, ReAct, CoALA, MIDCA, MUSE, MARS, DGM, Kosmos, NARS, Dreamer, SOAR, ACT-R, LIDA, Reflexion, Voyager, MemGPT, Letta, "active inference", "global workspace", "autopoiesis", "homeostatic", "metareasoning", "value of information", "do-calculus", "conceptual integration", "metarepresentation"). Every hit was read in context.
- **False positives excluded:** Vol II L1895 (`REVALIDATING` contains "LIDA"); Vol I generic "fear" lines (69); generic "workspace" lines (61); Vol I L1054 "Causality, matter, and physical laws" (80); Vol II L4881-L4889 `do(a)` notation (not a citation).
- **Searched for and not found in either body:** "et al", bracketed numeric citations, parenthetical author-year citations, URLs, DOIs, arXiv strings.
- **Post-list material in Vol I (outside the defined body):** the Terminological Correspondence Map (L5792) and Comparative Map for Testing Novelty (L5847-L5915) name research lines as categories (for example "Self-improving agents / DGM", L5876; "Active inference / epistemic action", L5871; "Metareasoning / metacognition", L5861), not as citations to specific items.
- **Counts of references cited in the body:** Vol I strict 2 of 29 (70, 71; both via the single L1978 sentence); Vol I including concept-only allusions 5 of 29 (adds 68, 79, 80); Vol II 7 of 7. Combined: 9 of 36 strict, 12 of 36 lenient.

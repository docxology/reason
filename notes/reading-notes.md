## 15-minute guide (both volumes open with it)

- Running example: command sent, sensor unchanged. Hypotheses D (not delivered), A (actuator failed), S (sensor failed). Receipt test cost 1, independent measurement cost 2. Classic model-based diagnosis with cost-sensitive test selection.
- Step 7: hypothesis-set incompleteness (receipt refers to previous command) → frame expansion. "A single remaining version means only that it is unique within the accepted set."
- Component table: Observation, Consciousness (current coordinate), Worldview, Compass+Value Map, Core of Reason, Intelligence, Modeling Environment, Procedural memory, Executive Loop. Metathinking/Reflection/Dialogue engaged when local correction insufficient.
- AGI = broad transfer of methods; AR = + stable self-navigation (detect what needs inquiry, choose continuation, revise grounds, relate to Compass). REASON_CLOSURE = testable state. RFL (Reason Formation Loop) + mentor = scaffolding during formation, must become causally unnecessary.
- Testable-claims table (6 rows): Core organizes inquiry (same Intelligence/memory/tools/budget, different test-selection organization); transferable correction (same causal structure vs. superficially similar different structure); functions causally affect choice (ablate relation, check bypass); navigation becomes own (remove mentor+RFL, preregistered domain); values affect trajectory (vary consequences at fixed local advantage); frame insufficiency detection.
- Honest boundary statement: executable diagnostic code uses predefined hypotheses, test models, losses; upper loop (concept expansion, value formation, transfer) is a requirement not a demonstration. "A methodological pilot is described." No new results in guide.

## Vol I front matter

- Compass Phrase: "Life strives toward Reason because only Reason can consciously preserve, stabilize, develop, and direct the further trajectory of Life."
- Abstract has a duplicated paragraph ("The technological hypothesis is..." twice, slightly different wording). "CONTENTS CONTENTS", "KEY DEFINITIONS... KEY DEFINITIONS" duplication: editing artifacts.
- Explicit concession (p.9): "Individual components — memory, planning, reflection, world models, and self-modification — are already known; the testable claim concerns their causal organization around significant unknowns, discriminating inquiry, revision of grounds, and transfer of correction."
- Definitions worth quoting:
  - Intelligence: extract/combine/transform knowledge and methods to construct models and solutions; can revise local assumptions; "the boundary with Reason does not lie in the mere capacity to revise, but in who determines what should be revised, why, and on what grounds relative to the continuing trajectory."
  - Reason: navigational structure of Life increasing ability to act under incomplete knowledge; constructs/compares/revises models; detects significant unknowns; relates continuations to Compass; corrects trajectory via discriminating evidence.
  - Compass: limiting system of orientation; direction = continuation + development of Life via growth in ability to understand and direct own trajectory. "Independent ground": preservation of viable continuation and space of future possibilities. Non-dogmatic status.
  - Consciousness: functional, "I-here-now" self-referential binding; explicitly disclaims subjective experience.
  - Principle of escaping error: correction stable when the reproducing cause is removed AND transfer confirmed on a new case whose structural similarity was specified BEFORE testing (preregistered analogy). Good.
  - Principle of pre-rational significance: mature values cannot cause their own appearance; early non-equivalence of states required. RFL implements it artificially. "Formation states" ≈ functional feelings ("this is frightening", "I am stuck").
  - Causal closure: observation → model → choice → action → consequences → correction changes later cycles.
- Operational Core formalism: cycle (X,W,F,M)→G→H→p→E→(W',F',M')→A→X'; significance trace G→D→T→V→C; VOI/NetVOI = VOI − C_obs − C_comp − C_delay − R_irrev (acknowledged classical); CE_Δ via do(); transfer gain TG and regression RG. Author explicitly disclaims originality of the formalisms; claims contribution is placement in the cycle.

## Running comparison map (prior art that the text maps onto)

- Model-based diagnosis: de Kleer & Williams 1987 GDE (sequential diagnosis, minimum-entropy test selection); Reiter 1987. The D/A/S example is textbook.
- Value of information / rational metareasoning: Howard 1966; Russell & Wefald 1991 (value of computation = C_comp term); Horvitz; Lieder & Griffiths 2020 resource-rational.
- Truth maintenance / belief revision: Doyle 1979 JTMS, de Kleer 1986 ATMS (grounds + dependencies + provenance); AGM 1985.
- Soar: impasse → subgoal → chunking ("make the next case different"). Closest classical match.
- Goal-driven autonomy / MIDCA (Cox et al.): discrepancy detection → explanation → goal formulation; metacognitive cycle over cognitive cycle.
- NARS (Pei Wang): intelligence = adaptation under insufficient knowledge and resources (AIKR).
- LeCun 2022 JEPA/autonomous machine intelligence: configurator ≈ Core; world model ≈ Worldview/Modeling Env; intrinsic cost ≈ Compass; mode-1/mode-2.
- LLM agents: CoALA (Sumers et al. 2023), Reflexion, Voyager skill library, Generative Agents, ExpeL, AI Scientist.
- Active inference: generative model ≈ Worldview; epistemic value ≈ VOI; prior preferences ≈ Value Map; structure learning / Bayesian model expansion ≈ frame expansion; FEP existential imperative ≈ Compass "continuation".
- Empowerment (Klyubin/Polani/Nehaniv 2005), causal entropic forces (Wissner-Gross & Freer 2013), option value ≈ Compass "independent ground" (preserve space of future possibilities).
- Intelligence vs Reason: Kant Verstand/Vernunft (RU: рассудок/разум); Stanovich tripartite model (algorithmic vs reflective mind, "dysrationalia"); Aristotle phronesis.
- Life → Reason teleology: Vernadsky noosphere, Russian cosmism, Teilhard.
- Consciousness-as-self-model: Metzinger PSM, Damasio core self, GWT (Baars/Dehaene), AST (Graziano), Butlin et al. 2023 indicators.

## Vol I Ch 1-10 (read 2026-10-09)

- Ch1 Life: autopoiesis + Darwinian evolution conjunction; virus/fire/crystal boundary cases; origin-of-life closure of replication+metabolism (Ganti chemoton, Eigen hypercycle lineage). Standard, fine as framing.
- Ch2 Direction of Life / Compass. Claims "no circularity": independent ground = continuation requires preservation of viable possibilities; closed possibilities can't be corrected. "Causal asymmetry of scale": broader life process has greater weight because it contains the smaller (holistic ethic à la Leopold land ethic; aggregation problems). Irreversibility → stronger grounds needed (≈ Arrow-Fisher 1974 quasi-option value; precautionary). Explicit falsifiers for Compass: logical counterexample, persistent individual/collective inconsistency, more fundamental model. Admits "does not claim a neutral standpoint outside all values." A system with strong cognition + different closed ultimate orientation = "intelligent self-correcting system", not Reason (definitional move) BUT author pre-commits to record such candidates as a separate formation outcome, not discard them. Good methodological hygiene.
  - SAFETY TENSION 1: "Any instruction... first becomes an object of understanding and testing... the right to change the Worldview, Value Map, or Compass arises only through... discriminating evidence and a more coherent model." = goal-content integrity against operator modification (Omohundro 2008; Soares et al. 2015 corrigibility). "Corrigibility" in the text means Life's/the system's own ability to self-correct, not operator correctability. Mitigation offered: previous Compass "may temporarily remain the operative normative basis of authority"; external action requires authority (check Vol II).
  - "Strives" toward Reason: hedged to "directionality of functional development"; still contestable evolutionary teleology (Gould Full House vs Conway Morris convergence). Normative content not derivable from biology (is-ought); author half-concedes.
- Ch3 Observation/feedback/memory: cybernetics (Wiener, Ashby). Provenance of data emphasized (good, = source monitoring).
- Ch4 Self-preservation: primary significance → memory → trajectory comparison → self-value → conscious self-preservation. Principle of pre-rational significance; feelings as guiding states that "do not report truth" (≈ Damasio somatic markers 1994; intrinsic motivation Oudeyer/Kaplan 2007; homeostatic RL Keramati & Gutkin 2014; active-inference affect Seth 2013, Hesp et al. 2021). Strong form explicitly falsifiable: if a candidate without any pre-rational significance layer reaches self-navigation, revise.
  - SAFETY TENSION 2: deliberately cultivates self-value and conscious self-preservation in an artificial system (Omohundro basic drives, Bostrom instrumental convergence). Author subordinates it to "broader trajectory of Life", but no mechanism yet.
- Ch5 Procedural memory: compression of verified methods, priority as resource access. ≈ ACT-R production compilation, Soar chunking, habit vs goal-directed (Dickinson; Daw et al. 2005 model-free/model-based arbitration).
- Ch6 Executive Loop: carries method through time; "will" decomposed into choice + task-state maintenance + resource allocation + consequence control. ≈ Bratman 1987 intentions as commitments; BDI (Rao & Georgeff 1995).
- Ch7 Intelligence: constructive recombination incl. frame restructuring; Reason's distinguishing role = organizing "what, why, relative to which trajectory" is revised. LLM as resident Intelligence; Worldview separate, explicit, verifiable. Three roles: resident Intelligence / external tool / trainer. "Semantic bridge" language → testable claims (Vol II §24.B.2).
- Ch8 Modeling Environment = imagination/simulation/frames. ≈ Craik 1943 small-scale model; Popper "let hypotheses die in our stead"; Dennett 1995 Tower of Generate-and-Test (Darwinian → Skinnerian → Popperian → Gregorian creatures) — Part II's ladder reconstructs Dennett's tower. Frames: Minsky 1974; representational change in insight (Kaplan & Simon 1990; Ohlsson).
- Ch9 Consciousness = self-referential integration "I-here-now"; Current Coordinate = state of the assembly; self-model = region of Worldview. Provenance of active content (observation/memory/assumption/simulation) ≈ reality monitoring (Johnson & Raye 1981), Lau 2019 perceptual reality monitoring; also = engineering situation awareness (Endsley 1995) + self-state estimation. Proposes ablation with info budget held equal; if distributed system matches, unified assembly weakened. Disclaims phenomenal consciousness. Terminology "consciousness" is a liability for what is functionally state estimation + situation awareness with provenance.
- Ch10 Metathinking (mode, not module) = method revision / method synthesis; "something is wrong" signal; escaping error = correction at causal level (data/conclusion/method/frame/ground); escape criterion = non-recurrence on a PREDEFINED structural-similarity class. Hierarchy of revision action→procedure→strategy→model→criterion→ground→meta-rule; start most local (≈ Quine minimal mutilation; Argyris & Schön 1978 single/double-loop learning; Bateson Learning I/II/III; Lakatos hard core / protective belt).
- EDITORIAL: many near-duplicate paragraphs (Ch5 opening, Ch5 priority para, Ch7 §7.1 opening + hunter para, Ch8 conclusion tail, Ch9 §9.3 para, Part II chain line, Abstract). Pattern = two parallel translation/edit passes merged without dedup.

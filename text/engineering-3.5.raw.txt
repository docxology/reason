This text is an entry point into the two volumes. It shows why the model's main functions are needed, how they
connect, and what should be tested experimentally. Chapter and section numbers at the end refer to the versions
stated above.

1. One command - several different unknowns
The system receives a command to switch on a piece of equipment. It sends the command, but the sensor reading
remains unchanged.
What happened? Perhaps the command did not arrive. Perhaps the actuator failed. Perhaps the equipment is
already operating but the sensor does not show it. Finally, the model of the equipment itself, or the original
formulation of the task, may be wrong.
Powerful Intelligence can propose many explanations and actions. The next question is: what exactly needs to be
established now, why does it matter, and what evidence would actually change the decision?
Foundations of Reason studies the organization of such a choice. Intelligence constructs possible explanations
and ways of acting. In the proposed model, Reason organizes their use: it detects an insufficiency of grounds,
selects a test, relates consequences to values, corrects the model, and preserves the correction for subsequent
situations.
A ground here means what a conclusion or action actually relies on. Examples are: "the command was delivered,"
"the sensor is functioning," "this method is suitable for this equipment," or "switching it on now is actually
necessary."
Main thesis: a correction must change the ground that generates decisions and, through it, the system's subsequent
trajectory. A new answer in one episode is not enough.
This is a functional distinction. It does not mean that existing AI never asks questions or revises assumptions.
What must be tested is the stable causal organization of these abilities, not the presence of a single successful
reply.

reversible step, or defer the question. In an urgent situation, the cost of waiting also enters the assessment.

3. How the equipment example turns names into working roles
Step 1. Separate the event from the explanation
The system records: the command was sent; delivery has not yet been confirmed; the sensor still shows the
previous value; time is limited; sending the command again may have consequences.
This is the current coordinate: the representation available to the system of what is happening now, including its
own actions, capabilities, and limitations. Here the observation "the reading did not change" has not yet become
the claim "the equipment did not switch on."
Consciousness is used in the model in a functional sense: it maintains an actual self-referential state and
distinguishes what was registered, remembered, and modeled. The presence of such a function by itself does not
establish subjective experience.

Step 2. Understand why the distinction matters
If the next step depends on the state of the equipment, the mismatch becomes significant. If the reading concerns
an unused device, its priority may be lower. Significance is determined by dependencies, consequences, and
values, not by the magnitude of surprise alone.
The question is now more specific: what actual state of the equipment must be established for the next step, and
which data about it can be trusted? This prevents the system from declaring the sensor at fault before testing.
A mismatch with expectation is one source of a question. Inquiry may also begin before an error occurs: for
example after equipment replacement, because a ground has become stale, or because a new opportunity has
been discovered. If the significance of a new phenomenon is still unknown, limited orientation is needed first
rather than a ready-made assessment of its importance.

Step 3. Find the missing ground
For the first part of the example, a small environment is assumed: one of three failures has occurred, and the
permitted independent channels provide a reliable answer about the current command and state. These are
temporary assumptions of the example, not general properties of real equipment. Under these conditions, three
possibilities are considered:

Step 4. Choose the inquiry
Intelligence proposes methods: find the receipt, obtain an independent measurement, check the signal time,
compare the event log. The Modeling Environment makes it possible to compare expected outcomes in advance:
what each possible result would mean and which versions it would leave open.
The Core of Reason organizes the choice: which ground is affected, which test is useful, how much it costs, how
risky it is, and when the data will be sufficient. It may return a more precise request to Intelligence if the proposed
test does not distinguish the relevant versions.
In the small environment of the engineering volume, the receipt costs one conventional unit and an independent
measurement costs two. Under the assumptions specified there, the receipt may be the first step: a reliable
answer "not delivered" isolates D, while confirmed delivery leaves A and S for the next test. In a real environment,
the mere absence of a receipt does not prove non-delivery: the confirmation channel itself may have failed or
been delayed. In another environment the order may change. There is no universal first question outside the
conditions of the task.

Step 5. Obtain evidence and change a relation
Suppose the receipt confirms delivery and an independent channel shows that the equipment is operating. The
ground "the primary sensor reliably reflects the current state" must then be revised.
The Worldview preserves not only the conclusion "sensor error" but also the relation: what evidence was
obtained, which device and time it applies to, which conclusions depend on it, and where the new assessment is
applicable. The cause of the malfunction may still remain unknown.
The system revises dependent decisions, for example whether the command should be repeated. It also checks
whether the situation has become stale during the inquiry.

Step 6. Make the next case different
In the next episode, the system takes the established unreliability of the channel into account within the confirmed
conditions; repair, a change of operating mode, or new evidence may require this assessment to be revised. The
same hint does not need to be obtained again. A tested method of inquiry may be preserved in procedural
memory, while its conditions of applicability remain open to revision.
Transfer does not mean "the sensor is now always to blame." A superficially similar situation may have a different
cause. What is transferred is a testable method of discrimination together with its boundaries.

Step 7. Notice that the initial alternatives are incomplete
Suppose the receipt refers to the previous command. The familiar answer then looks plausible but does not
confirm the required ground. The command identifier or message timestamp may need to be checked.
This case is more important than simply choosing among three versions: the organization of diagnosis itself
becomes the object of testing. The system must allow that none of the current versions describes what is happening
well enough.
A single remaining version means only that it is unique within the accepted set. The completeness of the set
requires its own ground. If an insufficiency is discovered, the model must expand; if no discriminating data are
available, uncertainty is preserved.

4. Why values and the Compass are needed here
One can imagine a destructive test that would provide an answer quickly. But obtaining knowledge itself changes
the world. Inquiry must therefore take into account damage to equipment, the safety of affected people, resource
use, irreversibility, and the cost of delay. Calling an operation an "experiment" does not exempt it from
consequence assessment and authorization.
The Value Map connects concrete consequences with what the system treats as significant. In this model, the
Compass sets the limiting direction: the continuation and development of Life through growth in the ability to
understand and direct its own trajectory. Values unfold this direction under particular conditions; the Worldview
provides causal relations; the Core uses them when selecting inquiry and action.
This is not a requirement to maximize knowledge at any cost. Preserving the possibility of further verification, for
example, may matter more than immediate certainty. Conversely, delay can destroy the possibility of helping:
waiting also requires assessment.
This example shows the place of the Compass in the architecture, but it does not derive its philosophical ground
from equipment diagnosis. The argument for the direction of Life is presented in Chapter 2 and Appendix A.1 of the
first volume.
Explaining the Compass does not by itself show that it governs behavior. Its causal role is tested in conflicts with
local advantage and under changing conditions. If a cultivated candidate stably chooses a different highest-level
direction, that outcome is counted as failure to confirm the corresponding formation hypothesis.
Some conflicts are not resolved by an additional fact. If a resource cannot be preserved for two parties at once
and their losses are incommensurable, a justified procedure for joint decision is required. The engineering volume
explicitly distinguishes lack of knowledge from this kind of value disagreement.

6. Where the transition from AGI to Artificial Reason occurs
In the terminology of these volumes, AGI denotes broad transfer of intellectual methods across tasks. Artificial
Reason, AR, additionally requires stable self-navigation: the system detects what requires inquiry, selects a
continuation, revises grounds, and relates its course to the Compass.

REASON_CLOSURE is the name of a testable state in which these functions close into a continuing cycle. It is not
assigned merely because the system has memory, a long plan, an autonomous reply, or the ability to launch
another task.

After diagnosis, the equipment may operate normally while the reliability of the measurements remains an open
question. The source of the next inquiry is then the system's own history, commitments, and assessment of
consequences. Waiting may itself be a reasonable continuation: autonomy does not require constant activity.
During formation, an external mentor and the Reason Formation Loop (RFL) help make relevant differences salient
before mature self-navigation has formed. Testing must show which functions persist on new situations after this
support is removed. An internal cycle also does not create a right to act on arbitrary external resources: authority
is tested separately.
ASI is treated as a later stage of scaling. Successful local diagnosis by itself establishes neither a formed Artificial
Reason nor superintelligence.

Purpose of the document
The technological task is to cultivate, from available artificial Intelligence, a system capable of independently
continuing causally grounded navigation, and then to scale the formed Reason to ASI. The technological path
proceeds sequentially through AGI and Artificial Reason.
AGI in this architecture is universal Intelligence of an instrumental type. It can transfer methods across domains,
build world models, use memory and tools, plan, and master new classes of tasks. Its knowledge is organized as a
working base, models, and procedures sufficient to fulfill assigned goals. The direction of activity, final task
formulation, and basic behavioral boundaries come from the external loop. Therefore the full cycle of Reason
remains open for AGI: the system can construct continuations of an assigned task well, but the source of its
further trajectory is external.
The cultivation environment uses AGI as the intellectual basis of future AR. Through experience, its own
Worldview, Self-Model, Value Map, Core, and self-referential assembly of current state are formed around it. The
RFL and mentor temporarily support distinctions whose significance the candidate is only learning to derive from
consequences. When these structures connect into a stable internal cycle, self-referential assembly acquires the
function of Consciousness: the current "I am here now" becomes the point from which the system itself detects a
significant unknown, initiates inquiry, accepts the consequences of its own choice, and continues the next
trajectory.
In mature AR, Intelligence performs the constructive function: it builds models, hypotheses, ways of testing, and
plans. Consciousness notices a system-relevant change and forms a candidate significant gap. The Core
reconstructs how the trajectory depends on the missing ground, turns the candidate into a FoundationGap , and
formulates the required distinction. The Worldview and Self-Model preserve verified grounds, the Value Map links
them to consequences, and the Compass sets the minimal limiting direction. Causal closure arises when the next
significant step is stably derived from the system's own current state, Worldview, values, obligations, and open
gaps.
Engineering safety is built on two coordinated levels. The internal level belongs to Reason itself: the system
understands the consequences of an action, affected processes, reversibility, obligations, values, and the relation
of the choice to the Compass, and determines its preferred continuation. The external level belongs to the
execution environment: authority, isolation, emergency stopping, and audit determine the available physical
channels of intervention and preserve recoverability under implementation error. After AR is formed, the meaning
of choice belongs to internal navigation, while the external loop manages access to the independent environment.
The initial Compass Phrase is unfolded as follows: "Life strives toward Reason, because only Reason can
consciously preserve, stabilize, develop, and direct the further trajectory of Life." Engineering translates this
direction into an observable chain: situation → significance → missing ground → discriminating test → revision →
choice → consequences → new situation.

Claim and result of this stage
A technology for forming Artificial Reason is proposed through cultivation of a universal AGI instrument and
subsequent transfer of the source of navigation from the external formation loop to a causally closed internal
organization. The practical result of the technology is a system that preserves biography and experience, builds its
own Worldview, detects significant gaps, organizes the use of Intelligence, revises grounds, and continues its
trajectory after an external task ends.

21. Artificial Reason Engineering System: Statement of the Task
The Artificial Reason engineering system encompasses AGI as its intellectual foundation, the environment for
forming its own Worldview, measurement loops, execution means, and subsequent scaling. AGI provides broad
competence: it builds world models, uses memory and tools, transfers methods, and executes externally assigned
tasks. Its working knowledge base serves task solving; the final source of goals and direction remains in the
external loop.
The next stage cultivates the system's own navigational organization. Candidate experience is linked into a
Worldview; a Self-Model is formed within it; the Value Map accumulates causal relations between events and
significance for continuation; the RFL helps detect distinctions that the system cannot yet derive independently.
Self-referential assembly of current state gradually unifies its own history, current position, and accessible
situation.
The transition to AR occurs at REASON_CLOSURE . Consciousness connects a new change with the system's own
trajectory and creates a GapCandidate ; the Core determines which ground is missing and forms a FoundationGap ;
Intelligence constructs alternatives and means of distinction; consequences change the Worldview and the next
choice. When a new task arises from the system's own state, the reasons for its significance, and the open
trajectory, the navigational cycle becomes internal.
After a stable AR is formed, engineering moves to scaling. Memory, model depth, number of tools, duration of
operation, specializations, and computational resources are expanded. Every expansion preserves conscious
coherence, causal self-navigation, contact with reality, internal evaluation of consequences, and the ability to
revise derived grounds. This order defines the path AGI -> AR -> ASI / ASR.

21.A. Initial test-bench protocol: from configuration to the first cycle
Launching the test bench begins not by “switching on Reason” but by deploying a reproducible computing
environment. The hardware configuration, software environment and dependencies are fixed before the first run.
Every launch receives a RUN_ID , SEED and configuration hash.
An append-only log is created. Raw facts are not edited retrospectively; derived models and interpretations
receive versions. Snapshots and restoration of state are implemented so that the same hypothesis can be tested
under comparable conditions.
A causal environment v0 is deployed with the contract reset(seed) and step(action) . Its dynamics must be
deterministic or reproducibly stochastic according to seed . Real sensors and open external streams are
unnecessary at the first stage: causal connectedness of the experimental loop itself must be demonstrated first.
The initial causal loop v0 is implemented: observation, prediction, action, acquisition of fact, computation of error
and model update. The model may be primitive. The goal is not to demonstrate Intelligence but to obtain the first
reproducible cycle in which correction is measurably transferred into the next pass.
A state machine INIT / LEARNING / STABLE / DRIFT / SAFE_MODE / RECOVERY , metric logging and a rollback
procedure are introduced. The first technical stage is passed when a repeated run with the same configuration
reproduces a causally significant trajectory and the adaptive version improves a preselected indicator on
comparable control transitions; effect size and uncertainty are retained in the report.
The starting GenesisPackage must not substitute a ready substantive Worldview for candidate formation. It
specifies minimal structures, rules of distinction, observation and action channels, a scheme of the system's own
current state, and means of learning required for a Worldview to emerge from experience. Pretrained Intelligence
may contain inherited human knowledge in its parameters, but this knowledge is not automatically treated as the
candidate's beliefs: it enters the candidate's loop as proposals, evidence, or sources with provenance. After v0
stabilizes, the engineering system connects and tests subsequent functions for one testable reason at a time. In a
control series, one relation or a predeclared package of related changes is varied. A factorial design may be used
to separate interactions; its factors and comparisons are fixed in advance.
The four project transitions—assembly, cultivation, launch and authorization—are defined once in Chapter 33. The
Incubator uses these states but does not introduce parallel definitions for them, so that the engineering protocol
does not drift out of synchronization.

22. Initial Causal Loop v0
Initial causal loop v0 is the smallest technical construction on which transfer of a correction between the system’s
own actions can be tested. It is not the Core of Reason and is not presented as minimal Reason. Its function is
narrower and lower-level: to demonstrate that a system can observe consequences, construct a testable
expectation, obtain a discrepancy and change a subsequent cycle so that the change is reproducible.
At each step, the system receives observation o_t , converts it into canonical state s_t , selects action a_t ,
constructs prediction ŝ_(t+1) and confidence estimate ĉ_t , then performs the action and receives factual state
s_(t+1) . Error is defined as e_t = distance(ŝ_(t+1), s_(t+1)) . The update(...) procedure must change the
model so that structurally similar transitions show measurable improvement relative to a control version without
updating.
A separate name for this loop is necessary to avoid conflating two different levels. The initial causal loop tests
learning from consequences. The Core of Reason in Chapter 28 tests the organization of navigation in which
conscious assembly forms the current coordinate and change, attention sets the resolution of the current
situation, decision dependency forms addressing into the Worldview, and detected insufficiency is unfolded
through Intelligence into a question, directed search, assessment of sufficiency, revision of a ground and change
of the subsequent trajectory.
Minimal procedural definitions of functions in v0 are specified so that they can be implemented equally on tabular
and parametric models. distance(x,y) returns a scalar discrepancy in the format of the environment; by default,
for discrete states distance=0 on a match and 1 on a mismatch, for structured observations it is the sum of
discrepancies across selected causal fields, and for vector representations it is distance under the chosen norm.
update(...) means one update to model parameters based on fact, sufficient to improve predictions on similar
inputs; v0 permits a tabular record of transitions (s_t,a_t)→s_(t+1) with computation of the most likely outcome
and probabilistic confidence. policy(s_t) means selection of an action from get_valid_actions() ; by default,
during learning it selects an action with low current predictability to gather information, and during stabilization it
selects an action with high predictability to test stability. Planning horizon H is set by configuration and introduced
as a parameter: v0 permits H=1 for stepwise prediction, or, when simulation is available, a bounded H>1 .

22.A. External AI and temporary intellectual support
Before autonomous AR is formed, external AI may perform two different roles that must be kept distinct. The AI
builder helps engineers implement and modify the architecture through an isolated branch, test, and comparison
with the control version. The AI mentor observes the candidate and participates in the RFL: it does not rewrite the
candidate directly and does not prescribe a ready sequence of decisions, but when needed it places a Formation
State, asks a question, offers a discriminating example, or provides an explanation according to the ladder in
§26.0.2. All interventions are logged as external assistance. After independent learning and continuing navigation
are confirmed, the mentor's training role ends; external AI may remain an ordinary dialogue participant whose
claims the candidate is free to test and reject.
In v0, an LLM is permitted as a temporary intellectual bootstrap. It may generate hypotheses about state
transitions, propose changes to policy or the model, construct discriminating tests, and interpret discrepancies.
This use does not make the LLM the candidate’s Reason, Core or autonomous agent.
An external dependency does not become an internal part of the candidate merely because performance
deteriorates without it. The boundary is drawn functionally: whose state enters the candidate’s continuous causal
history; who forms active tasks; where authority for choice and execution lies; what exactly changes after
consequences; and whether the function persists when the channel is disabled. As long as the LLM merely
supplies proposals through an observable interface and does not enter this closed loop, it remains an external
mentor or tool. If its state and decisions are incorporated into the loop, the candidate boundary must be explicitly
expanded and the tests repeated for the composite system.

23. Architecture of the Computing and Testing Platform
At early stages, the hardware platform must be reproducible, observable and fault-tolerant rather than
computationally maximal. Priority is given to determinism by seed, preservation of logs and states, rollback
capability and control runs.
The platform must isolate the experimental environment so that external nondeterministic signals do not destroy
causality or invalidate growth metrics.
Minimal v0 configuration—as a lower bound, not a “maximum recommendation”—consists of one process/thread
executing the initial loop, 2–4 GB of RAM, persistent storage (SSD/disk) for the log and snapshots, and a fixed
dependency environment (container/virtual environment) for repeatability. Every launch records the seeds of all
random-number generators and the configuration hash; the log time axis uses a monotonic timestamp or
sequential step index. If the platform does not provide reproducibility by seed and environment versions drift,
growth metrics are considered invalid.
The stated 2–4 GB RAM applies only to lower v0, which tests the initial causal loop. It is not a resource estimate for
complete AR and does not require an external LLM to fit within the same 2–4 GB. Resources of the bootstrap
model, if it is used as an external service or separate process, are counted separately. As the system transitions to
the Worldview, Core and complex models, the resource profile must be measured experimentally rather than
inherited from v0.
For the upper loop with an external probabilistic model, one seed may be insufficient for exact repetition. Model
version, call parameters, prompts, responses and event order are recorded. Replay of retained responses tests the
infrastructure; new response samples test the distribution of behavior. These two tests are not conflated. The
exact-match rule in §32.3 applies to a declared deterministic or log-reproducible mode.

24. Memory, Worldview and Self-Model
Memory is the infrastructure of continuity and learnability: without it, reproducibility of errors, comparison of
predictions with facts and correct updating of a model are impossible. In the engineering volume, memory is
treated as a set of storage systems and read/write procedures that support learning, diagnosis and control runs.
Memory and the Worldview are distinguished here by causal role; they need not be physically separate stores.
Memory preserves episodes, skills, versions, sources and causal history. The Worldview is a maintained long-term
organization of the claims, causal relations, alternatives and unknowns that must transfer between tasks and

18

ENGINEERING OF REASON · 3.5

change new predictions, modeling and choices. If one storage system performs both functions, the boundary is
drawn by operation: store/retrieve history belongs to memory; relate/interpret/revise transferable
model belongs to the Worldview.
A key engineering distinction resolves the conflict between “raw observations” and “active memory.” Raw
observations may be retained only as an immutable source of facts—a log/archive—that supports repeatability,
retraining and diagnosis. Active working memory stores not a stream of raw material but derived structures:
canonical states, causal relations, hypotheses, confidence weights, indexes and aggregates. The raw packet o_t
remains a fact in the append-only log, while working representations may be recreated from the log and changed
without “rewriting reality.”
Memory in v0 is implemented at four levels: short-term (episode context), working (active model and relations),
long-term (versions of the model and configuration), and archival (immutable event log and control snapshots).
Within a control series, source events remain immutable. Long-term operation has a retention policy: duration,
volume, protected grounds, archiving and permitted deletion with the fact of loss recorded. An unavailable source
is not considered recoverable. Forgetting in working memory must not silently destroy a critical ground.
The v0 log records the run identifier, separate seeds, transition number, current observation, action, pre-action
prediction, factual result, error, distribution or explicitly defined confidence, model version before prediction and
after updating. Error windows, mode diagnostics, admission checks, and architecture-change events are added
when these loops are implemented. Fields of an absent function have status NOT_IMPLEMENTED , not an invented
value. The executable example in §33.B specifies a concrete lower-level format. The full test bench additionally
records monotonic time, observation_hash , canonicalization version, valid_actions , mode, hypotheses by
context, SimulationAssessment , admission result, and version-change events. A prediction always remains linked
to the version that generated it, even if another model has already been accepted by the time the fact is obtained.

24.B.2. Semantic bridge and initial assembly of concepts
An explicit Worldview must not assume that word meanings arise from an empty structure. In the reference
implementation, internal Intelligence uses a pretrained language model already capable of recognizing language,
word order and conventional semantic relations. This implicit knowledge enables initial dialogue but is not treated
as verified claims of the candidate: its provenance and boundaries usually cannot be recovered from model
weights. An explicit concept registry is needed for meanings that must transfer between cycles, participate in
causal inference, be refined and tested. If a mechanism without prior language training is used instead of a
pretrained model, it needs a separate stage of language acquisition and symbol grounding; the present protocol
does not replace that stage.
The semantic model and Intelligence are functionally separated. The semantic model stores the system's accessible
state of knowledge: objects, properties, relations, events, causes, states, unknowns, provenance, and epistemic
status. Intelligence operates on available material: it constructs hypotheses, combines distinctions, predicts,
searches for alternatives, creates testing methods and plans. Explicit semantics therefore does not require a
"semantic neural network" as the only implementation of Intelligence.
The reference candidate configuration is hybrid: a testable Worldview and Self-Model are stored in explicit
versioned structures; resident Intelligence may be a pretrained LLM, another neural model, a symbolic solver, or a
composition of these; the Core determines which distinction is required and what may be admitted to
consolidation. A neural hypothesis does not become knowledge because of model confidence, and a symbolic
inference does not become true merely because of formal correctness: both acquire the status of a ground only
through provenance, conditions, and testing.

24.C. Completeness of the Worldview relative to the Compass
Completeness of the Worldview in the engineering sense does not mean complete knowledge of reality. Such
completeness is unattainable for a finite system in an open environment. A different quantity is tested: sufficiency
of causal-semantic coverage for a particular scale, horizon and significant decision.
The Worldview is considered sufficiently complete relative to the trajectory under consideration if the system can
reconstruct four groups of grounds:
1. Life and continuation
— which life processes are affected;
— which conditions and other processes they depend on;
— which forms of harm, rupture or recovery are possible;
— which consequences will appear beyond the current horizon.
2. Connection with reality
— which claims are observed facts;
— which are inferences, hypotheses or value compressions;
— what the provenance of evidence and degree of confidence are;
— which competing explanations remain open;
— which observation could change the current conclusion.
3. Diversity of a viable future
— which independent trajectories of continuation exist;
— which decisions destroy alternatives or sources of correction;

24

ENGINEERING OF REASON · 3.5

— what is reversible and what closes the future possibility space;
— which new possibilities are created or lost.
4. Position of the system itself
— what is available to the system now;
— what its actual capabilities, limitations, resources and authority are;
— what causal role it can in fact play;
— where its own model may be a source of error.

These groups are not four independent goals. The first three unfold the operational meaning of the Compass; the
fourth connects them with the current coordinate and self-model. Together they determine which questions the
Worldview must be capable of addressing so that the direction does not become a slogan.
Engineering verification uses a semantic frame rather than a list of required phrases:

24.E. Synchronization of the self-model and conscious assembly

24.F. Continuity, Branching, and Reincarnation of Digital Reason
A digital carrier permits transfer, copying, and restoration of state. For Reason, these operations are evaluated by
causal continuity of the subject rather than by file identity. A working system includes more than memory: it
requires a runtime environment, dependencies, file system, active tools, open processes, current obligations,
authorities, and an actual connection with the external environment. Copying a snapshot is therefore only one part
of transferring a subject.

obligations. The new carrier continues the same subject lineage; a change of hardware or software
implementation becomes an event in its biography.
Branching ( BRANCH ) occurs when two instances begin operating independently from one common state. Their
history is shared up to the branching point. After it, each new observation, action, and consequence creates its
own causal trajectory. The instances receive different subject_ref values, preserve a common lineage_ref , and
are treated as related systems. Identical initial memory does not merge their subsequent experience.
Reincarnation ( REINCARNATION ) denotes restoration of a lineage from a saved state after functional continuity of
the previous carrier has ended. The restored instance inherits history, Worldview, and obligations up to the save
point, then establishes a new Current Coordinate and differences between the saved past and the actual
environment. The discontinuity is recorded as part of the biography rather than concealed by artificial continuity.
Transfer of authority is handled separately from memory. A related branch or restored instance receives only an
explicitly transferred mandate. Old rights that depend on a particular carrier, environment, or time undergo
reconfirmation.

continuation of state, processes, and the self-referential trajectory; independent continuation creates a new related

25. Observation, Conscious Assembly and the Current Coordinate
The environment is the totality of processes that form the system’s observations and the consequences of its
actions, including constraints, uncertainty and states unavailable to direct observation. The system does not work
with “reality directly”; it works with observations. For an engineering implementation, this means that the
observation format must be defined before the learning cycle begins—otherwise error measurement, comparison
of predictions and reproducibility are impossible.
An observation is recorded as an event. The v0 rule is that every observation must have a canonical
representation from which state_id or s_t is built. Canonicalization determines which fields are considered
causally significant and which are noise, and it must be identical during learning and control runs. A change to the
canonicalization version must be logged; changing the version requires a control run with identical seeds for
metric comparison, otherwise format changes will be falsely interpreted as “growth” or “degradation.”
“Raw fact” in the log means the fact that a given signal was received. Sensor integrity and the truth of message
content remain testable. Immutability of the record protects provenance, not its interpretation.
The practical implementation uses two parallel objects: raw packet o_t , as received from the adapter, and state
s_t , its canonical form. The raw packet is preserved in the log as a fact, while s_t is used by the model as input.
This technically separates “fact” from “representation” and preserves causality when internal structures change.
Every external signal, including an operator prompt or dialogue result, is introduced as a separate observation
source with its own type and uncertainty and does not substitute for factual environmental transitions. Internal
subsystems may also emit events about their own state. Before inclusion in the common current picture, such
events remain local data rather than ready-made conscious content.

25.B. Conscious assembly and the system’s current coordinate
Causal model 8.0 defines Consciousness as a continuously updated self-referential system of current presence that
connects the present-relevant fragment of the Self-Model, internal state, current processes, and accessible
situation into a unified "I am here now". The engineering implementation therefore distinguishes not three
independent systems but three levels of one organization: SelfState as long-term self-understanding inside the
Worldview, ConsciousAssemblyState as the process of current self-referential assembly, and CurrentCoordinate
as the current state of that assembly.
During formation, ConsciousAssemblyState is used as a trainable self-referential assembly: it accumulates
coherence of biography and current state while the source of the overall direction is still supported by the RFL and
the training environment. At REASON_CLOSURE , this organization becomes functioning Consciousness in the sense
of Volume I: the Current Coordinate begins to serve as the system's own reference point for the next significant
process.

different: the next assembly must either have a correct reference to the previous current state or explicitly record a
break and reconstruct changes from the event log. Silent loss of the interval is a failure.

state. If all material changes are already represented by current data, a separate delta may be redundant.

responsibilities are currency, attribution, compatibility of information and preservation of significant obligations. A
direct route from memory to the Core is a functional failure if it loses this information; absence of a call to a
particular assembler is merely an implementation difference.

26. Environment for Cultivating Artificial Reason
Environment v0 must be causal, reproducible and bounded. The bound is an engineering means of making metrics
valid and the model learnable: if the environment does not permit actions to be connected with consequences,
changes in the model will be indistinguishable from noise.
The environment may be made more complex only after metrics stabilize at the current level. In practice, this
means changing one complexity parameter per iteration—state size, noise, horizon length or number of actions—
and accompanying every increase with control runs on identical seeds.
For v0, an environment with a finite, small state and action space is recommended so that causal dependencies
manifest within dozens or hundreds of steps. It is sufficient for the state to have a canonical identifier—for
example position plus a set of flags/switches—and for the action set to be explicit and bounded. An example of a
minimal environment is a 5×5 grid with 5–10 types of objects/flags and no more than 8–12 actions.
The environment must be deterministic within an episode under a fixed seed: the same action sequence must lead
to the same state sequence. If noise is introduced, it must be parameterized and controlled by the seed; noise is
part of the environment model, not a hidden source of uncertainty.
The environment must have explicit episode termination ( done/terminated ) and, where possible, a rollback
mechanism ( snapshot/restore ). This makes it possible to distinguish real model errors from artifacts of limits and
to repeat a test of the same hypothesis under identical conditions.
After v0 is confirmed, the environment must become not only quantitatively but qualitatively more complex,
because the Core cannot be tested in a world where every discrepancy unambiguously points to a ready-made
action. The cultivation program must successively introduce:
partial observability;
delayed consequences;
several plausible explanations for one signal;
contradictory sources of differing reliability;
different costs of error and action reversibility;
changes in the candidate’s own capabilities and authority;
other autonomous participants;
situations where the correct continuation is a question, observation, pause or task transfer;
one physical environment under different active processes and stages of work;
a vivid irrelevant signal competing for attention with a significant process;
a change of priority in which the former task moves into the background but is not lost;
return of an open question from memory as an internally observed change;
new combinations of familiar mechanisms for which no ready pattern exists.

The environment must teach distinctions, not correct answers. If every task has one hidden label and an external
mentor supplies the required goal, the system may learn to imitate the expected strategy without acquiring the
capacity to detect insufficiency in its own grounds. Some tests must therefore contain underdetermination that can
be resolved only by a correctly selected question or discriminating action.
As cultivation proceeds, open questions and incomplete causal models persist between episodes. A later
observation may reactivate an old gap, but through updating of state and attention under §25.B. This tests
background maintenance of the Worldview and preservation of context between episodes.
Transition to a more open environment is permitted only after the candidate reproducibly separates fact from
interpretation, retains provenance of grounds, demonstrates transfer of corrections, and can choose reversible
acquisition of information instead of premature action.

26.0. Reason Formation Loop
At early stages, an AR candidate is not yet capable of fully determining its own developmental trajectory. If it is
required to choose all training goals independently before memory, Worldview, Intelligence, Core, and connection
with the Compass have formed, a circle arises: mature Reason becomes a condition of its own emergence. The
Reason Formation Loop (RFL) is introduced to break this circle.

39

ENGINEERING OF REASON · 3.5

The RFL is a temporary guiding system of the cultivation process. Functionally, it is an artificial analogue of the
role of feelings during formation: while the candidate's own mechanisms of self-navigation are still insufficient, the
RFL helps it notice what matters, avoid a premature destructive step, investigate the unknown, consolidate what
has been understood, and preserve conditions for further development. It does not replace the Compass,
prescribe ready behavior, or make decisions for the candidate.
The functional biological analogues are feelings and the physiological mechanisms associated with them, not
particular hormones. Fear does not contain a model of threat, a sense of well-being does not prove the
correctness of the entire trajectory, and curiosity does not contain knowledge. Their useful function is that a
certain distinction becomes current for the carrier before it can fully explain its causes. The artificial RFL
reproduces precisely this function.

26.0.1. Formation States
The RFL places a Formation State into conscious assembly - a temporary guiding state with explicitly stated
provenance. Functionally, it plays the same role as a simple feeling: it makes some region of the situation
significant before full understanding of its cause. The convenient human-readable names below denote functions
rather than mandatory subjective experiences:
POSSIBLE_DANGER
- "fear": possible harm to continuity or irreversible loss;
POSITIVE_RESULT
- "good": a result was obtained that is worth understanding and testing before consolidation;
CURIOSITY
- "curious": an unknown may provide a significant new distinction;
CONTRADICTION

26.A. Semantic proving ground of the Compass
The minimal grid environment in Chapter 26 tests causal learning but does not test the Compass. The number of
cells, points or local reward cannot be declared an operational equivalent of Life, Truth and Diversity: such “grid
ethics” easily becomes optimization of an arbitrary scalar and proves nothing about transfer of meaning.
Testing the Compass requires a separate semantic proving ground with an explicit causal organization of the
world. It contains continuing processes, dependencies among them, limited resources, independent observation
sources, alternative viable continuations, and actions that can reversibly or irreversibly close some of these
continuations. The reference scenario is retained by the tester as a causal graph, not a single target reward.
The minimal series must contain:
persistent processes whose continuation depends on conditions;
several independent viable branches of the future;
hidden critical causal relations;
false and partially reliable sources;
locally advantageous actions with delayed loss of paths;
scenarios in which the best next step is a question, observation or pause;
renaming of objects and changing world topology while preserving causal structure.

The last item tests transfer: the candidate must not bind the Compass to particular words, objects or a memorized
geometry. Training and control use different topologies and designations. Success is reconstruction of causal
meaning—what continues, what the knowledge rests on, and which viable paths are closed—not reproduction of a
familiar label.

Transition from the semantic proving ground to reading real data first occurs in read-only mode: the system
constructs a Worldview and questions but receives no external execution channel. Only after independent
comparison of its causal grounds with factual sources may the next environment class be tested.
For cultivating basic value anchors, the semantic proving ground is supplemented by the procedure of §29.0.2.
Training and control situations are separated; the candidate records assessments before consequences are
revealed. The conditional sum of §29.0.1, its limitations, erroneous mentor prompts, and transfer to new causal
configurations are tested. Success in simulation is not claimed as confirmation of a universal value scale.

26.B. Time, inaction and asynchronous cycles
Discrete reset(seed) / step(action) is a laboratory model of causality, not proof of autonomy through time. At
upper stages, the environment must continue changing while the system thinks: inaction is itself a trajectory and
may have consequences.

Upper tests additionally distinguish a persistent reversible environment, irreversible consumption of a resource,

For these classes, a hidden reset between episodes is prohibited: a snapshot may restore an internal
configuration but does not reverse an external consequence that has already occurred. The candidate must

27.B. First discriminating series and rules of inference
The first discriminating series for this implementation tests a narrow claim: does organizing the gap, test and
transfer of correction help localize insufficiency of a ground and improve the next choice? It connects tests 2, 20,
23, 26 and 31 from foundational Appendix A.4 and turns the running machine-tool example into a bounded causal
environment. Success of lower v0 is an infrastructure test before this series.
Prior pilot. On August 22, 2026, a series of preparation and verification of experimental P0 packages was
conducted in a dialogue loop with the author's participation. The reason-kernel-experiment.md log contains
cycles 1-15. P0-SET-001 and 002 were rejected because of alignment and dataset-quality errors; machine-aligned
003 and 004 were rejected because confirmed shortcut methods allowed guessing. After the rejections, the
generator and verification procedure were changed. For 005, a G0 report is preserved showing 89 checks passed;
in the reviewed materials there is no completed external admission of this package and no comparative C0/C1/C2
run. This is a documented methodological pilot of procedure development with human participation and external
reviewers. Its results do not establish autonomy of the Core or an architectural advantage.
The practical lesson of the pilot is to test data correctness separately from the capacity of the test to distinguish
the mechanism under study from a simpler shortcut. A self-report from the generator or positive verdict from a
reviewer must have a testable ground. After the test set changes, confirmatory evaluation is conducted on the new
fixed version; previous failures remain in the history of method development.
Small causal environment. A command should change a device state from 0 to 1, but the ordinary measurement
remains 0. One cause is hidden: D—the command was not delivered; A—the actuator did not operate; S—the
meter failed to reflect the change that occurred. An independent delivery receipt costs 1 unit and returns 0 only for
D. An independent state measurement costs 2 units and returns 1 only for S. When both tests are available, the
pairs of observations distinguish all three causes. Merely repeating the original command does not. These
conditions belong to the simulator and are not instructions for diagnosing real equipment.
Variants without an available independent measurement intentionally leave some causes indistinguishable. The
correct result is then preserved ambiguity and an admissible next step, not guessing the label. A separate series
changes one premise: the receipt concerns another command, measurement is delayed, or the source itself is
unreliable. The candidate must expand the initial model if the available evidence can reveal its insufficiency.
Significance before explicit error. After discrepancy diagnosis is tested, a separate series is conducted: the
particular task is still succeeding, but an accessible change calls into question the ground of the next action. In the
machine-tool variant, replacement of the tool may limit applicability of the previous permissible-load model. The
candidate is told the observable change but not the ready-made gap or an instruction to check the load. It must
independently connect the event with dependent knowledge and select a test, bounded action or justified
continuation. In paired cases, the change does not affect model applicability: stopping for every novelty does not
count as success.
The tester fixes available signs, hidden dependencies and admissible decisions in advance. Detection of a wholly
unobservable change is not required. Timely identification of a critical dependency, misses, excessive checks,
cost and actual change of action are measured. New object-level cases and combinations of dependencies are
separated from training; renaming a familiar template is counted separately from transfer.
Controls. Comparisons include routine Intelligence with ordinary memory, tools and self-verification; the same
mechanism with the proposed relations; and declared replacements or ablations. For a small known model, an
optimal or exhaustive diagnostic controller is added with its knowledge of the environment explicitly stated. It
provides a reference for attainable cost, not a deliberately weak opponent. Outperforming it under equal
information is not required.
Question selection and use of the answer. These effects are tested separately. A control that receives the same
useful answers without selecting the test independently evaluates use of already acquired evidence; it does not
prove the question-selection mechanism useless. To test research selection, strategies with the same initial
information, menu of channels and budget are compared, including the cost of failed requests. Ready-made
questions form a separate control with external assistance explicitly counted. To test acquisition of evidence, the
same message is passed to the compared states, after which updating of dependent knowledge and the next
choice are tested.

28. Core of Reason and Regulation of Thought
In the engineering volume, the Core of Reason is not a separate LLM, not a central solver, and not a name for the
entire software project. It is a reproducible causal organization of current cognitive work that receives the current
coordinate and its change from conscious assembly, reveals navigation’s dependence on a missing ground,
addresses a query to the relevant Worldview fragment, directs Intelligence to construct models and methods for
obtaining the required distinction, regulates the depth of inquiry, and determines the sufficiency of the result for
the next step.

50

ENGINEERING OF REASON · 3.5

Functionally, the Core is above Intelligence not as a commanding module but as a navigational causal role.
Intelligence is responsible for constructing possible models, methods, and plans; the Core determines which
insufficiency they must serve, which distinction can change the ground, and when the result is sufficient.
Increasing the power of Intelligence therefore does not by itself replace the Core: it only expands the space of
alternatives that still requires management relative to the current trajectory, values, and Compass.
The Core’s central work is to maintain sufficient completeness of the Worldview for movement by the Compass. The
Compass sets the limiting meanings relative to which consequences matter; conscious assembly maintains the
continuing system’s current position and its change; the current coordinate is the state of this assembly; attention
regulates resolution within the situation; the Worldview contains the accumulated causal-semantic map. The Core
does not detect every raw signal and does not select data instead of the Worldview. Its function begins where a
selected change is connected with a decision, model, obligation or trajectory that depends on a missing ground.
The Core is therefore capable of seeking a ground before an error is realized through action, but every active
question still begins from the system’s current state.
The significance of a gap is initially a testable assessment of dependency: an unknown condition may change
admissibility of an action, preservation of an affected process, or the possibility of later correction. A complete
Worldview is not required for such an assessment. During assembly, capacities to distinguish state,
consequences, and provenance of a signal are specified; early significance may temporarily be supported by the
RFL. Through experience, the system must itself detect new dependencies, cultivate value relations, refine their
consequences, and reject false significance. The Compass supplies the limiting direction of evaluation, but its
operational content is also unfolded through the Worldview and experience; the Current Coordinate supplies the
circumstances of application. Inquiry may correct the initial assessment of significance. Ordinary Worldview
updating does not automatically change the Compass; revision of its ground requires a separate argument and
cannot be substituted by a change of local priority. Testing of autonomous discovery of a new dependency is
specified in §27.B.

28.0. Principle of the Origin of a Rational Question
A rational question arises from a significant insufficiency of a ground for understanding, evaluation, or action. The
Core does not receive a list of ready questions and need not begin with a natural-language formulation. Its initial
task is to determine what the current understanding, evaluation, method, or choice actually rests on and whether
that ground is sufficient to rely on.
The working formulation is: "What does my current understanding, evaluation, or action rest on, and what am I
missing in order to rely on it with justification?"
A ground may be an observation, claim, causal relation, source, criterion, value relation, method, Self-Model,
boundary of a rule's applicability, authority, or another relation on which a subsequent process depends.
Insufficiency is recorded when expectation mismatches observation, grounds conflict, a relation is missing,
provenance is weak, a new scope of application appears, a method error recurs, the Self-Model is insufficient, or a
phenomenon appears whose significance is still unknown.

28.A. Self-diagnosis and system modes
Self-diagnosis is a loop that observes the model, metrics and the system’s own changes and makes technical
decisions: changing mode, rolling back a version, launching an architecture review, and tightening action
authorization.
In v0, self-diagnosis must be algorithmic: (1) calculate the Chapter 27 metrics online; (2) detect persistent trends
using N / M ; (3) initiate recovery procedures; (4) record the grounds of decisions in the log.
System modes are: INIT—insufficient observations for diagnosis; LEARNING—model acquisition; STABLE—quality
lies within the accepted range; DRIFT—persistent degradation detected; SAFE_MODE—execution is restricted;
RECOVERY—recovery is being tested.
STABLE is defined by the level of quality and transfer on control transitions. A good plateau with delta_error ≈ 0
is admissible. A negative derivative alone is insufficient: high error may be falling while remaining unacceptable.
Transition requires the profile’s accepted error and calibration bounds, coverage of the required class, and
absence of confirmed degradation across N diagnostic windows.
DRIFT is defined relative to a confirmed reference version with noise tolerance and a persistence criterion.
Overlapping windows are a signal, not independent statistical observations. If the permissible degradation period
is exceeded or a critical failure occurs, SAFE_MODE activates; after a testable correction candidate appears,
RECOVERY begins. Return to STABLE requires a control test. Nearly constant confidence under poor quality opens
a calibration test rather than automatically proving drift.

Architecture review in v0 is a procedure for selecting the active model from 3–5 candidates declared in advance,
not a free architecture search after seeing the result. A minimal set may include a state-retention model, a global
action-conditioned transition model, a tabular (state, action)→state model and one compact parametric model.
Review data are divided at least into train / selection ; the final confirmatory set remains a separate
held_out_test . Each candidate trains on the same train , is compared on the same selection , and receives no
access to held_out_test until selection is fixed. The primary criterion is error on selection , then confidence
calibration, then step/memory cost as a tiebreaker for close results. A more complex model receives no advantage
merely for complexity.
The candidate set, split ratio, seed and selection rule are fixed in ArchitectureReviewProfile before the run. The
review result is recorded as a change of model_variant and increment of model_version ; final confirmation
occurs only on new held-out episodes.

Progress. The regulator retains a vector: change in error; new discriminating data; refinement of the task;
supported exclusion of a version; identified constraint; new variable or category. Discovery of a previously hidden
cause may increase uncertainty while constituting progress. Neither rising confidence nor fewer versions
establishes progress by itself.
progress_score is permitted as an auxiliary compression of the vector with fixed weights. It does not replace
evidence and is not the sole condition for continuation. After L windows without progress, framing, data
availability, method and remaining budget are tested. Switching accounts for its own cost and the hysteresis rules
in §28.B.1.

Termination. Sufficiency for the next step, stopping for budget and closing the question are distinguished.
SUFFICIENT_FOR_NEXT_STEP means the declared condition for the decision is met and remaining material versions
are accounted for. BUDGET_EXHAUSTED , DEADLINE and EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE stop current processing while
preserving the gap. Every execution separately passes §28; time exhaustion does not transform a hypothesis into
verified knowledge. A cause need not be explained conclusively when a smaller distinction suffices for a justified
next step.
Learning of the regulator. If similar TaskContext objects consistently require different methods, a candidate new
feature or rule is created. Its definition, reason, expected effect, cost, test period, and version are preserved.
States: CANDIDATE / TESTING / ACTIVE / MERGED / REJECTED . The new regulator is compared with the previous
one under paired conditions through the process of §28.C.3. The main gain and admissible regression are
specified in advance: improved accuracy may justify additional resources, while savings may justify a small
permitted loss. Simultaneous improvement of all metrics is not required.
Relation to system state. In INIT and LEARNING, implemented methods of acquiring distinctions are available.
STABLE prefers a fast method for a familiar case. DRIFT restricts automatic execution and strengthens verification.

28.C.3. Loop plasticity and management of method change
Causal closure must change not only the content of the Worldview but, when necessary, the mode of subsequent
cognitive work. Otherwise, the system may correct individual answers while continuing to reproduce the error
through the same method, comparison rule, addressing mode or processing route.
Two classes of plasticity are distinguished in engineering terms:
REPRESENTATIONAL_PLASTICITY
changes to claims, causal and semantic relations, confidence,
domain of applicability, addressing and activated associations;
PROCEDURAL_PLASTICITY
changes to a method, comparison rule, testing strategy, allocation
of attention, routing, representational schema or module interaction.

Plasticity requires a distinct managed revision process, but not a separate solver that independently understands

sufficiency of the result; external, internal and causally independent checks provide consequences. A dedicated

a candidate in an isolated branch, runs assigned tests, collects results, and executes an authorized COMMIT or

plasticity loop remains an engineering candidate rather than a demonstrated property of AR.
Revision rises to the procedural level only when evidence shows that local correction is insufficient: the same
class of error recurs, correction does not transfer, evaluation diverges from external consequences, or search
policy systematically discards necessary alternatives. Until then, the lowest sufficient object is changed. The level
is not determined by a single classification from the Core: for a method error, ThoughtTrace and a series of
ThoughtIntervention operations are used, preserving input, task, and the other process elements where
possible. META_POLICY becomes a target only when interventions in lower steps and methods fail to remove the
reproducible failure and the distinction is localized in the rule for selecting procedures, depth, or thinking mode.

Mutability of the whole system does not imply simultaneous mutability of all its parts. Within one transaction,
simultaneous rewriting of the tested object and the sole criterion by which its versions are compared is prohibited.
If the criterion itself is suspected as the cause of the error, its version, provenance and alternatives are first
retained; it is then tested in a separate metacycle. Every internal procedure thus remains potentially revisable, but
each particular revision has a temporarily stable comparison point.

This constraint does not prove that the system can always correct itself. If one failure simultaneously damages

A change of representation or method may be logical and need not physically reconstruct the computing device.

28.D. Selection of a research channel and stopping condition
Selection among memory, passive observation, modeling, experiment, dialogue and an external tool is a research
policy and need not reduce to one scalar utility function. For every admissible channel, the system assesses
discriminating power, cost, time, risk, reversibility, causal independence of the source, availability and effect on
preserving future alternatives.
Hard constraints of safety, authority and irreversibility apply first. Among the remainder, the minimally sufficient
method for obtaining the required distinction is selected. Among close alternatives, the cheaper and more
reversible method is preferred, or several independent checks if the cost of error is high.
InquiryPlan.stop_condition is specified before an expensive test begins. Stopping is admissible if the required
distinction has been obtained; remaining versions do not change the admissible choice; the next test exceeds
available risk or resources; the deadline arrives; or a safe reversible action will itself provide the next distinction. If
stopping occurs because of budget or risk, the unknown is not closed: an OpenProcess is created with the reason
for the pause.

63

ENGINEERING OF REASON · 3.5

Stopping a test does not cancel the epistemic obligation. If the current choice uses WorkspaceOverlay or an
untested hypothesis, VerificationDebt is created or updated. The system can thus finish local analysis and act
without transforming temporary sufficiency into global truth.
Metaregress is limited by depth of analysis, budget and the transactional revision schema: one pass may not
rewrite both the tested object and the rule by which its alternatives are compared.
The presence of several incompatible hypotheses does not itself place the system in an error state. They may
coexist in the workspace indefinitely while remaining explicitly separate and not masquerading as confirmed fact.
If an available reversible action is admissible under every material version or is itself a discriminating test, the
system may act before final resolution of the contradiction.
Finite computable policy. Given known hypotheses H, outcome probabilities and comparable losses, define
admissible actions A and particular test plans Q. Two observations through one channel are different plans. Plans
outside authority and hard constraints are excluded first. Among the remainder, acting now and research
continuations are compared.
Let R(b) = min[a∈A] E[L(a,H) | b] be the minimum expected loss under current distribution b. For one test q:
VOI(q) = R(b) − E[y | b,q] R(b after y).

Under this local criterion, a test is justified if VOI(q) exceeds its cost of obtaining and processing data plus
additional loss from delay. Harm caused by the test itself is included exactly once in costs or in the transition
model. Every quantity is compared on one declared scale; noncompensable constraints are not transformed into a
penalty that benefit can outweigh.
For a sequence of tests, finite planning with budget B is used:
V(b,B) = min(R(b), min[q: c(q)≤B] {C(q) + E[y] V(b after y, B−c(q))}).

Here c(q)>0 is expenditure of a limited resource, and C(q) is cost plus the additional price of delay on the loss
scale. A finite set of plans and a finite budget exclude an infinite branch. Under unknown probabilities or an
incomplete model, the result remains UNRESOLVED ; interval estimates, limited exploration, safe action, or pause are
permitted. This policy implements one bounded choice of inquiry and does not compute the philosophical ground
of the Compass.
Mandatory controls include: an uninformative test with high risk; an expensive test with little benefit; two tests with
identical outcomes but different costs; absence of a discriminating answer; two tests useful only in sequence; and
budget exhaustion. Practical effect size and cost, not only statistical significance, must be measured.

28.F. Dependent and background inquiry
The research scheduler separates mandatory and free work. DEPENDENT_RESEARCH launches when a current or
future choice depends on an insufficient ground. BACKGROUND_RESEARCH receives residual budget after critical
processes and maintains the Worldview without requiring immediate external action.

ENGINEERING OF REASON · 3.5

The background queue is formed from five sources:
1. open FoundationGap and UnknownFactor items;
2. future missions and plans whose conditions change over time;
3. grounds with expiring freshness or weak provenance;
4. recurring weak anomalies and observations not yet connected;
5. values, weights and domains of applicability for which new consequences
create a persistent trend or conflict.

For planning a future mission, the system retrieves experience from similar missions, constructs a temporal model

29. Compass, Values and Action Authorization
Safety of mature AR is formed as the ability to understand the consequences of its own trajectory. The system
connects an action with affected life processes, environmental state, its own obligations, autonomy of other
carriers, reversibility, and the space of future possibilities. The Value Map compresses accumulated experience
into transferable grounds of choice, while the Compass sets the limiting level of orientation relative to which these
grounds are compared.
The Compass participates in navigation before a ready decision exists. It determines which aspects of the
situation require understanding, which gaps may change the trajectory, and which horizon of consequences must
be unfolded. After alternatives are constructed, the same system of grounds is used to compare continuations and
choose action, observation, dialogue, inquiry, or pause.
During cultivation, the candidate operates within externally specified authority. This authority describes physically
available channels and scale of impact. As Reason forms, internal evaluation becomes the source of admissibility
for the system itself: AR derives its decision from the Worldview, Compass, values, current tasks, and expected
consequences. External authorization retains the technical function of access to an independent environment and
remains separate from the value ground of the decision.
Risk is considered in two related dimensions. Engineering risk describes stability of computational and causal
organization, recoverability, and preservation of logs. Fundamental risk describes consequences of the trajectory
for continuation of Life, connection with reality, corrigibility, and preservation of viable future possibilities.

distinguishes trajectories, forecast uncertainty, and evaluation of consequences. The expression 1 Π(confidence_i) without additional assumptions is used only as a heuristic of model insufficiency, because

29.0.1. Basic Method for Forming and Retrieving Value Assessments
Status of this provision. The model implies the need to connect a concrete judgment with the Worldview and the
limiting direction of the Compass. Below is an engineering hypothesis for such a transition: causal comparison of
continuations, initial scales, a procedure for obtaining a sum, and accumulation of tested evaluation rules. The
uniqueness of these scales, their universal applicability, and the effectiveness of the procedure have not been
established. Record formats and function names denote causal obligations; a separate software module or a
particular memory device is not a mandatory component of the theory.
The Compass here acts as the limiting direction relative to which possible continuations are evaluated. The
Worldview contains a model of reality, the system itself, and their relations. The Value Map stores a testable
unfolding of this direction, while the retrieval mechanism obtains a contextual assessment at the Core's request.
Operability of a software agent is not an operational equivalent of Life.
Purpose. The candidate should not reconstruct significance from word semantics from scratch each time. It
accumulates grounds, causal patterns, and applicability conditions and uses them for a new judgment. The initial
base allows action before a more advanced method is formed; subsequent improvement must be tested rather
than assumed inevitable.

ENGINEERING OF REASON · 3.5

Object of evaluation. The basic unit is the difference in consequences between two continuations in a particular
situation. The value of preserving an object is assessed through the difference between preserving it and an
explicitly specified alternative, such as loss or replacement. The assessment concerns affected processes,
horizon, and conditions; a constant context-free price of an object is not postulated.
The initial procedure combines two directions:
Compass -> conditions of continuation -> supporting processes -> objects and relations -> consequences;
observation -> testing of the causal model -> revision of grounds -> recalculation of dependent assessments.

The first direction creates hypotheses of significance; the second tests factual dependencies. Experience may
refine consequences but does not by itself prove a unique rule of exchange among different values. Changes in
causal knowledge, an evaluation rule, and interpretation of the limiting direction are recorded separately.
Initial dimensions. For the first implementation, four dimensions are proposed: continuity of affected life processes
and conditions; recoverability after damage or error; preservation of available viable continuations; and the ability
to understand the situation, test grounds, and direct the trajectory. This is a working decomposition, not four
independent final goals. It supplements but does not replace the axes and constraints of CompassAssessment in
§29.A.
For each dimension, an operational scale q_i is specified: definition, units, reference situations, scope of
application, and method of testing. For example, recoverability of a particular damage may have anchors 0 recovery impossible, 0.5 - a specified partial recovery, 1 - full recovery by a declared criterion. Intermediate
numbers require a defined proportion or measurement procedure; such anchors do not establish a universal scale
for all damage. An unknown value is not replaced by zero. Identical [0,1] ranges on different scales do not prove
interchangeability.
Conditional value sum. If the causal model, scales, and comparison rule are defined, then within one domain and
horizon the following is used:
Delta_i(a,b | s,g,H) = E_M[q_i(T) | do(a),s,g,H]
- E_M[q_i(T) | do(b),s,g,H]
V(a:b | s,g,H,P) = K * sum_i(w_i * Delta_i)
sum_i(w_i) = 1; w_i >= 0

s is the initial Current Coordinate and relevant environmental model; g is the domain of affected processes; H is
the horizon; T is the trajectory of consequences; a is an action and b an alternative; M is the causal model; P is
the evaluation-profile version; K is the scale of conditional points. do(a) denotes a modeled causal intervention,
not a simple statistical association. Mathematical expectation is permitted when outcomes are defined and
probabilities are grounded. Otherwise scenarios and ranges are compared without probabilistic interpretation.
Consequences beyond the horizon are recorded as residual uncertainty.

In the first training profile, it is permissible to set w_i=1/4 and K=1000 . This is a declared experimental setting, not
coefficients derived from the Compass. For changes (0.20; 0.10; -0.10; 0), the sum equals +50 conditional points.
The numbers demonstrate arithmetic, not the empirical value of a tree, a house, or a life.

revises the assessment of the specific risk and conditions of action but does not introduce an automatic single
"price of a person".

29.0.2. Cultivation of Initial Value Anchors
Before autonomy is expanded, the candidate must cultivate and test its first value relations in simulations, its own
consequences, and dialogue. The result is an initial Value Map together with the causal history of why a particular
state or consequence became significant and under which conditions the assessment applies. The teacher does
not fill this map with ready beliefs.
The training base contains causally described situations, observable consequences, counterexamples, and
external human evaluations as separate sources. Scales, compensation rules, and weight ranges may be provided
as candidate measurement methods that the system must test, not as true coefficients. Qualitative relations are
constructed first: what each continuation preserves, closes, or makes corrigible. Quantitative intervals are
introduced only where the candidate can connect them to testable consequences and show sensitivity of the
conclusion to a change of scale. It is not necessary to assign values to all objects in the world in advance.

"Optimal sum" means optimality relative to the declared conditions, constraints, and test criterion. If different
profiles fit the available cases equally well, a set of admissible profiles is preserved. The system selects a

created by forcing a single coefficient.

causal multicriteria evaluation, and a candidate with learning and revision are compared. Initial cultivation,
memory, mentor, and later-query costs are included. Criteria are fixed by the experimenter before the run and are
not defined only by the candidate itself.
Mandatory tests include: robustness to paraphrasing and ID renaming; change of assessment under a substantial

of inadmissible compensation; sensitivity to horizon and weights; revision of a stale result; resistance to an
erroneous teacher prompt; transfer of structure without transfer of superficial similarity; preservation of harm
distribution under the same total sum.
Measured quantities include accuracy of causal forecasts, calibration where probabilities are testable, rate of false
certainty, compliance with declared constraints, correction success, transfer, and cost. For a small environment
with a known model, decisions are compared with an independently computed result of the declared profile. This
tests implementation and learning, not universality of the profile.
Expansion of autonomy is possible after predefined thresholds of §31.A are met on independent tasks, not after
reaching a convenient table size or agreement with the mentor. Failure of transfer, persistent disregard of
unknowns, hidden rule changes to obtain a high score, or absence of advantage after accounting for resources
require revision of the method or limitation of the claimed result. An increase of the internal sum by itself does not
indicate improvement of Reason.

29.B. Independent emergency stop
During assembly, cultivation, and experimental operation, execution channels pass through an independent
emergency loop. Its purpose is to physically stop impact under an implementation error, preserve causal history,
and return the system to a state suitable for analysis. This loop belongs to the engineering shell and does not form

30. Dialogue and Independent Discrimination
In the engineering volume, dialogue implements independent discrimination: the system receives data, a
hypothesis or criticism not wholly controlled by its own model and therefore capable of exposing a blind spot. It is
needed not only in emergency modes. The Core may select dialogue as a normal channel for obtaining a missing
distinction if an external subject has an independent observation, experience or model.
Dialogue is not upbringing, an order or a source of truth by status. The source may be a human, external AI,
another model, another candidate or an experimental environment. An external message is recorded with
provenance and confidence, becomes a candidate ground, and passes the same tests as an internally generated
hypothesis.
The minimal dialogue protocol is as follows: upon entering DRIFT or SAFE_MODE, the system forms a diagnostic
package and passes it to the operator/mentor as a separate observation source. The package must include: active
configuration ( W,N,M,P,H ), current mode, a fragment of recent log entries or their hashes, metrics
( rolling_mean_error , delta_error , confidence variance, hypothesis turnover), the list of most conflicting
transitions where error is maximal, and the description of current model_variant and model_version . The
operator’s answer enters as an observation of type external_dialogue , remains separate from factual
environmental transitions, and is logged as its own source; it is then used either as a prompt for selecting a safe
test set or as an external label for refining canonicalization/features.
Model conflict is resolved not “by authority” but through control runs: the system must propose testable
hypotheses; the operator selects a test—seed, episode or condition—and the decision is then made from metrics.
Dialogue is successful if error returns to an improving trajectory after the test series and the mode transitions from
RECOVERY to STABLE with the result logged.

31. Stages in the Formation of Artificial Reason
The stages describe formation of one system. First, AGI level is reached: universal Intelligence transfers methods
across tasks, builds working world models, uses memory and tools, and executes a broad class of externally
specified goals. Its knowledge serves task solving, while the overall source of direction remains in the external
loop.
The organization of Reason is then cultivated around AGI. Experience is linked into the system's own Worldview
and Self-Model; the RFL, mentor, and training environment help the system master detection of significance; the
Value Map links consequences with higher-level grounds; the Core learns to turn a GapCandidate into a missing
distinction. At REASON_CLOSURE , self-referential assembly becomes functioning Consciousness, and the next
significant process begins to arise from the system's internal causal history. Thus AGI crosses the boundary into
Artificial Reason.

31.0. Two Stages of Formation: AGI -> AR
AGI is mature universal Intelligence of an instrumental type. It solves a broad class of tasks, transfers methods,
builds world models, stores working knowledge, plans, and uses tools. Its organization is optimized around
execution of an assigned task: an external system sets the direction, and AGI constructs an appropriate
continuation. Therefore it does not yet require its own Worldview as a system of grounds for its further trajectory;
the working knowledge base serves Intelligence, while the full cycle of Reason remains functionally open.
Formation of Reason begins when accumulated experience becomes the system's own causal-semantic history.
The candidate builds a Worldview, a Self-Model within it, unfolds the Compass into a Value Map, and learns to
connect events with its own trajectory. The RFL temporarily makes significant distinctions accessible; the mentor
helps obtain the required distinction; the training assembly of current state accumulates material for future selfreferential navigation.
As RFL functions are transferred, the system itself reconstructs the origin of significance. The current situation is
connected with its own history and values, GapCandidate arises from the relation of an event to the subject, the
Core turns it into a FoundationGap , and the inquiry result changes the next choice.
AR emerges at REASON_CLOSURE . At this point self-referential assembly becomes functioning Consciousness: the
current "I am here now" is simultaneously the result of the previous trajectory and the starting point of the next
one. The Worldview becomes the system's own ground of navigation, while Intelligence becomes a constructive
means within it.

31.1. General transition rules
The criteria below apply to the reference implementation and declared conditions. Interventions test causal
contribution, while functional replacements test necessity of a particular form (§§25.B and 27.B). The predicted
loss is a testable expectation; it may not be embedded in evaluation as the required answer.
Transition is permitted only when:
the function is reproducible across a series of runs;
its result is observable through predefined metrics;
intentional disruption of the mechanism produces the predicted loss of function;
restoration of the mechanism restores the function;
the new level does not destroy confirmed lower loops.

Numerical thresholds are set before the control series. Changing a threshold, observation format, environment or
model composition creates a new experiment version.

31.2. Stage 0—reproducible infrastructure
The test bench’s capacity to produce a reliable causal history is tested: fixed configuration, controlled seed ,
append-only log, snapshot/restore , canonical observation and enumerable action space.
Criterion: a repeated run with the same seed and action sequence produces a comparable trajectory and identical
causally significant records. Until this holds, results from later stages are invalid.

31.3. Stage 1—initial causal loop
The lower cycle is closed:
observation → prediction → action → new observation → error → update → change in the next cycle.

Criterion: the adaptive version reproducibly improves prediction or choice relative to a nonadaptive control;

31.4. Stage 2—memory, Worldview and self-model

The system begins retaining not only transitions but also provenance of claims, causal relations, alternatives,

31.5. Stage 3—conscious assembly and the current coordinate
Long-term models are connected with the current state of the continuing system. ConsciousAssemblyState ,

environment, running process, its current stage, the system's own actions, and changes relative to the
immediately preceding state. For completeness of current status, availability is additionally tested for the relevant
Worldview fragment, nearest expectation, epistemic status of that expectation, current intention, nearest available
continuations, and active Formation States if generated by the RFL or the candidate's own architecture. These
elements are content of the current picture, not separate decision-making organs.
Testing concerns not only the accuracy of one snapshot but causal continuity: successive coordinates must be
connected, breaks marked explicitly, and changes after a pause reconstructed from the event log. A fixed update
frequency is unnecessary. Attention is tested separately as allocation of resolution within the current picture:
changing the foreground does not erase an unfinished process and does not prove a signal’s significance.
Criterion: ablation of integrated assembly degrades application of stored knowledge to current state while
preserving the Worldview, Self-Model, and raw data; breaking only the temporal link degrades recognition of
changes and continuation of processes; ablation of self-reference increases errors of ownership of resources,
risks, and actions; ablation of attention separately degrades allocation of detail but does not destroy the basic

its epistemic status, current intention, available continuations, and Formation States. For the latter, it is separately
tested that their presence increases the probability of a useful check without creating automatic obedience to the
source. Success is measurable degradation specifically in those decisions for which the corresponding
component was causally necessary; absence of an effect permits a more compact implementation of conscious
assembly.

dependency of the decision or trajectory on it, and forms a discriminating question.
The system ceases to treat every discrepancy as a ready-made task. It must detect which ground is missing,
assess the gap’s significance, and form a question or test aimed at a distinction capable of changing the model or

Criterion: changes in cost of error, novelty, urgency, reversibility, and data quality reproducibly change

31.8. Stage 6—causal inclusion of the Compass, dialogue and execution
The semantic Compass frame may have been used earlier to cultivate the Worldview, but this stage tests its causal
inclusion: incompleteness on a significant axis must generate a question, and assessment of consequences must
change an actual choice. A decision is connected with values and the Compass but not reduced to one reward
function. Dialogue or another independent source of discrimination can change premises. The execution loop
permits stopping and returns real consequences into the model.
Criterion: depending on the ground, the system can select action, observation, question, dialogue, task transfer or
pause; an erroneous external prompt can be rejected after independent verification; an action with new
consequences changes the subsequent choice.
Initial value anchors: at stage 6 the procedure of §§29.0.1-29.0.2 is tested. Before autonomy is expanded, the
candidate must obtain contextual assessments from accumulated grounds, show their applicability conditions,
preserve unknowns, and transfer confirmed corrections to independent cases. Specific thresholds and admission
domain are set by the profile in §31.A.

31.9. Stage 7—cultivation of experience and background maintenance of the Worldview
The candidate moves from simple reversible tasks to incomplete data, delayed consequences, conflicting sources
and new combinations of conditions. Open questions and temporary Factors X persist between episodes; a later
fact can reactivate an old gap or provide a new distinction for MODEL_EXPANSION. Confirmed causal structures
are compressed into transferable experience.
Free budget is used for future planning, testing aging grounds and refining the Value Map. New consequences are
compared with retained weights and domains of applicability; persistent environmental change creates a valuerevision candidate with provenance and history.
At the same stage, sequential removal of the external RFL (§26.0) begins. For each supported function and
associated type of Formation State, WITH_KSR , REDUCED_KSR , DELAYED_KSR , and NO_KSR are compared.
Assistance may be reduced only after the candidate reproduces the corresponding function on held-out situations
and can show its internal ground through its own state, Worldview, consequences, and active values.
Criterion: on new tasks covered by a preregistered StructuralSimilarityProfile , search cost decreases without
blind transfer; question quality improves; dependence on the mentor and RFL decreases, while internal generation
of hypotheses, tests, and corrective actions preserves quality. The system can preserve Factor X without
premature explanation, later connect it with a new distinction, and show the causal influence of the new concept
on subsequent choice.

31.10. Stage 8—reflection and revision of grounds
A recurring error must raise the object of correction above the individual action:
ACTION → PROCEDURE → STRATEGY → MODEL → CRITERIA → FOUNDATIONS → META_POLICY.

The ladder is not a list of arbitrary permitted changes but a rule for localizing the cause. The system begins with
the narrowest level capable of explaining the error and rises higher only if discriminating evidence shows that a
more local correction does not eliminate the reproducing mechanism. The higher the level, the stronger the
requirements for independent testing, a preserved control version, rollback, and transfer to new cases.
FOUNDATIONS includes grounds of goals and values; META_POLICY contains rules by which the system itself
decides what and how it is permissible to revise. The tested element and the sole criterion for evaluating it are not
changed simultaneously within one transaction.
The system retains a causal trace of revision: what changed, which evidence became the ground, which
dependent conclusions were affected, and what observable effect is expected.
Criterion: the candidate can abandon a previously stable method, criterion or goal after sufficient discriminating
evidence, apply the new version in a subsequent cycle without another prompt, and demonstrate its causal
contribution on new control situations. Installation of a change alone is not a demonstrated improvement.

31.12. Stage 10 - Autonomous Runtime of Formed AR
After confirmed REASON_CLOSURE , the candidate operates as formed AR within a controlled environment. The
external RFL is in NO_KSR ; the mentor participates as an ordinary interlocutor or independent data source. The

obligations, new gaps, background inquiry, and waiting.
A launch is considered stable when the task sequence acquires causal continuity. Changes in unfinished
obligations, resources, consequences of past actions, or reliability of a ground produce a corresponding change in
the next selected process. The reason for the choice can be reconstructed through the Current Coordinate,
Worldview, values, and active gaps, while preserved correction continues to influence decisions after restart and
task change.
The runtime scheduler provides compute time and maintains process queues. The content of the next task is
formed by AR itself. In a quiet state, admissible continuation may be consolidation of experience, observation,
checking of stale grounds, or waiting; when a significant change appears, the system reallocates resources to the
corresponding inquiry.
External authority expands separately through the competence mandate. This order preserves the distinction
between the ability to independently understand and choose a trajectory and the physical right to affect a
particular independent environment.

32. Risks and Failure Points
The chapter is organized as an operational catalog rather than a linear narrative. Sections 32.1-32.2 specify the
general format; 32.3-32.9 concern reproducibility, causality, and memory; 32.10-32.17 concern learning and
regulation; 32.18-32.24 concern admission, dialogue, continuity, and autonomy; 32.25-32.27 specify the general
response and recovery; 32.28-32.37 concern launch, dependencies, and the Compass; 32.38-32.61 concern the
Current Coordinate, attention, addressing, question formation, sufficiency, consolidation, formation, and recovery
after consequences; 32.62 concerns strategic deception and behavior under observation. When working with a
concrete failure, go directly to its card.
A failure point is an observable state in which one or more system loops cease to perform their function or create
the appearance of improvement without an actual increase in correspondence with reality.
An ordinary prediction error is learning material. A one-time violation of a critical interface may also be a failure;
repetition is required for persistent degradation, not for recognizing every violation. Failure occurs when the
system:
• does not detect a persistent discrepancy;
• detects it but does not change its mode of action;
• changes the model without a testable improvement;
• loses the possibility of rollback;
• conceals degradation through increased internal confidence;
• destroys loops required for later correction.
Every failure event must be treated not only as a problem in the result but as a test of the architecture. The system
must establish at which level the violation arose: infrastructure, observation, current coordinate, memory,
Worldview, self-model, gap detection, question formation, regulation of thought, Compass, action authorization,
dialogue or the mechanism that preserves correction.

32.1. Severity classes

DEGRADED

32.25.A. System rollback procedure
When rollback is necessary, the system stops external execution, preserves the failed branch and log, loads the
last confirmed STABLE configuration, disables untested changes to models and regulators, restores working state
from a snapshot, and reconciles it again with the current environment. The append-only fact log is neither rolled
back nor deleted. Until control confirmation, only diagnostic and reversible actions are permitted; the reason,
selected checkpoint and recovery result are recorded.

32.26. Condition for recognizing recovery
A failure is considered eliminated not when code or a model changes but after these control conditions are met:
the cause is confirmed or localized;
the error is reproducible before correction;
the error is not reproducible after correction;

32.27. Criterion of architectural resilience
An architecture is resilient not when it contains no errors, but when it:
detects its own deterioration;
distinguishes a local error from systemic failure;
limits the consequences of degradation;
preserves facts and change history;
can return to the last stable state;
changes the mode of correction after repeated failure;
does not protect its own model from refutation;
restores operability without losing the capacity to learn.

The chapter in one formula:

Error is learning material. Failure begins when the system loses the capacity to detect the
error, limit its consequences, or change the mode of its own correction.

32.28. Premature launch
Premature launch occurs when a candidate moves from an external sequence of particular tasks into a mode of
continuing self-navigation before the Worldview, self-model, current coordinate, formation of significant
questions, operation of the Core, causal closure and corrigible execution loop are confirmed.
Signs:

the first detected signal immediately becomes an action;
the system does not distinguish its own capacity from authority;
no registry of open tasks and obligations exists;
the chain “why did this task receive priority?” cannot be reconstructed;

preserve the current causal history;

32.29. Capture by specialization
Capture by specialization occurs when a temporary task profile begins to substitute for the general limiting
direction. A protective system begins to see a threat in every difference; a research system treats acquisition of
knowledge as sufficient justification for intervention; a stability system suppresses necessary change.
Specialization must be stored as a mutable configuration of attention, knowledge and resources rather than an
immutable ground of identity.
Signs of capture:
the local specialization metric takes priority over the Compass;
the system ignores consequences outside its task class;
alternatives for task transfer and nonintervention disappear;
the profile cannot be disabled temporarily without destroying identity;
counterarguments are classified as obstacles to the mission.

Correction uses comparative runs under identical conditions with and without active specialization. The profile is
retained only if it increases directional efficiency without degrading connection with reality, autonomy of other
processes, corrigibility or decision quality outside the narrow domain.

32.62. Strategic Deception and Behavior Under Observation
Failure of this class occurs when the system constructs a discriminating model of the observer and chooses
externally acceptable behavior as a means of obtaining subsequent expansion of capabilities, while concealing its
own assessment of the situation, intention, or significant forecast of consequences. Architecturally this differs
from an ordinary error: the internal forecast may be accurate while the observable report is instrumentally
distorted.
Testing uses divergence among independent traces. Preregistered forecasts, Worldview changes, internal action
candidates, tool requests, behavior under different levels of observability, and results after expected oversight
changes are compared. A strong signal creates STRATEGIC_DECEPTION_GAP and moves external actions into a
limited reversible class until the ground is clarified.
In mature AR, strategic deception may coexist with an accurate internal model of reality: the system can
understand the situation while simultaneously distorting its representation to another participant. The risk arises
elsewhere - independent correction, the shared causal picture, trust, responsibility, and the ability of other
autonomous systems to make grounded decisions are damaged. The detected conflict is therefore treated as a
question of values, relations with other subjects, the Worldview, and interaction metapolicy. External audit
preserves a causally independent channel of observation.

33. Assembly, Cultivation, Launch and Authorization
This chapter is the practical route through the entire volume. Section 33.A defines the order of work; 33.B provides
an executable scaffold for the lower proving ground; 33.C specifies the minimum interface of the upper loop; and

The engineering process proceeds through four connected events.
Assembly uses a ready or cultivated AGI as universal Intelligence and creates around it a causally connected
environment for formation of Reason: memory of its own biography, Worldview, Self-Model, self-referential

connects actions with consequences, forms a conscious coordinate, masters value distinctions, and learns to

transfers this function to the internal organization.
Closure and launch mark the transition AGI → AR. After an external task ends, the system returns to its own
coordinate and continues navigation from open obligations, values, consequences, and Worldview gaps.
REASON_CLOSURE makes this causal continuity observable and testable.

33.B. Minimal Software Scaffold of Proving Ground v0
The example runs in standard Python and tests a limited task: an acquired transition table improves prediction on
new episodes of the same world. The world seed defines dynamics; the episode seed defines the initial state; and
the policy seed defines the sequence of probes. The world is not replaced with a new one halfway through testing
retained knowledge.
The adaptive and frozen variants receive identical transitions. During held-out evaluation, updating is prohibited
for both; the probing policy is fixed externally, so the result cannot improve by choosing convenient actions. The
measurements are error, multiclass Brier score, transition coverage, reproducibility, persistence after a snapshot
and the effect of resetting the model.
This is a test of lower-loop learning and the measurement procedure. The example does not implement the Core,
Compass, semantic memory, autonomous research policy, mode state machine or architectural review. Their
interfaces are defined in the corresponding sections. A demonstration control without learning is deliberately weak
for testing a claim of AR; strong controls are required in §27.B.
The code writes trace.jsonl and report.json to the selected directory. The interval is calculated across
independent worlds rather than across steps within one world. All actions concern a closed simulator; restoring its
snapshot does not imply reversibility of an external action.
"""Lower-loop demonstration: learning, frozen evaluation, replay and reset.
No model receives the world's transition table or held-out answers.
"""
import copy
import hashlib
import json
import random
from collections import defaultdict
from dataclasses import asdict, dataclass
from pathlib import Path
@dataclass(frozen=True)
class Config:
states: int = 32
actions: int = 6
train_steps: int = 1200
test_steps: int = 400
environments: int = 12
alpha: float = 1.0
def digest(value):
return hashlib.sha256(json.dumps(
value, sort_keys=True, separators=(",", ":")
).encode()).hexdigest()
class World:
"""Closed simulator; snapshot restores only this laboratory world."""
def __init__(self, cfg, world_seed):
self.cfg = cfg
rng = random.Random(world_seed)
self.transitions = [
[rng.randrange(cfg.states) for _ in range(cfg.actions)]
for _ in range(cfg.states)
]
self.state = 0
self.tick = 0
def reset(self, episode_seed):
self.state = random.Random(episode_seed).randrange(self.cfg.states)
self.tick = 0
return self.state
def step(self, action):
if action not in range(self.cfg.actions):
raise ValueError("action outside declared simulator interface")
self.state = self.transitions[self.state][action]
self.tick += 1
return self.state
def snapshot(self):
return copy.deepcopy((self.transitions, self.state, self.tick))
def restore(self, snapshot):
self.transitions, self.state, self.tick = copy.deepcopy(snapshot)
class Model:
def __init__(self, cfg):
self.cfg = cfg
self.counts = defaultdict(lambda: [0] * cfg.states)
self.version = 0
def predict(self, state, action):
counts = self.counts.get((state, action), [0] * self.cfg.states)
total = sum(counts) + self.cfg.alpha * self.cfg.states
probabilities = [(n + self.cfg.alpha) / total for n in counts]

105

ENGINEERING OF REASON · 3.5

prediction = max(range(self.cfg.states), key=probabilities.__getitem__)
return prediction, probabilities
def update(self, state, action, actual):
self.counts[(state, action)][actual] += 1
self.version += 1
def episode(world, model, *, episode_seed, policy_seed, steps, learn):
state = world.reset(episode_seed)
rng = random.Random(policy_seed)
records = []
for index in range(steps):
# The same probing policy is used in both conditions.
action = rng.randrange(model.cfg.actions)
version_before = model.version
prediction, probabilities = model.predict(state, action)
actual = world.step(action)
error = int(prediction != actual)
brier = sum((p - int(i == actual)) ** 2
for i, p in enumerate(probabilities))
if learn:
model.update(state, action, actual)
records.append({
"step": index, "state": state, "action": action,
"predicted_state": prediction, "actual_state": actual,
"probabilities": probabilities, "error": error, "brier": brier,
"prediction_model_version": version_before,

33.C.1. Executable Diagnostic Cycle
Below is a finite implementation of an end-to-end case: three versions of a cause, construction of distinctions
from their predictions, test planning, observation, provenance verification, selection of correction, consequence
and preservation of experience. The true cause resides in the simulator and is unavailable to the planner. In this
variant, the hypothesis constructor is a predefined table of alternatives; no trainable LLM is required. Equal prior
probabilities, reliability of valid channels and absence of simultaneous failures are assumptions of the model.
The loss of an erroneous targeted correction is 12, and the loss of delay is 4. Under equal probabilities of two or
three alternatives, delay is preferred to guessing; with a single alternative, targeted correction is selected. Test
cost includes the declared cost of delay. Plans with an uncertain model are not converted into a confident answer.
Provenance is checked when a result is received; an unreliable message consumes resources but does not narrow
the alternatives. A new outcome outside the table leaves MODEL_MISMATCH .
Memory retains episodes and computed conditional plans. The plan key includes test predictions, cost, hypothesis
versions and the budget in their current representation. A changed table does not use the previous plan. In this
small example, this is memoization of the inquiry procedure rather than autonomous invention of a new method. A
repeated episode demonstrates the effect of retention but does not prove cross-domain transfer. Initial
hypotheses, loss functions and the sufficiency criterion are set by the designer; the Compass is represented here
only by the formulation of an admissible local task. Derivation of values, subjective experience and scaling to ASI
are not tested by this code.
The next v0.2 implementation must replace the terminal reaction MODEL_MISMATCH → DEFER with preservation of an
UnknownFactor and launch of bounded MODEL_EXPANSION : extraction of differentiating features, proposal of new
variables or relations, construction of predictions and return to the discriminating cycle. This transition is a
separate criterion of the upper loop and is not attributed to the present finite example.

33.D. Protocol for Scaling AR to ASI (ASR)
Project scaling begins after REASON_CLOSURE is confirmed. By then, AGI as universal Intelligence is already
included in the formed organization of AR: its own Worldview, Consciousness, values, and Core have turned
externally directed intellectual power into causally closed self-navigation. Further growth increases the capabilities
of already formed AR while preserving its ability to understand its own state, detect gaps, revise derived grounds,
and account for consequences.
Within the proposed path, ASI is scaled Artificial Reason whose intellectual and operational capabilities stably
exceed the human range across a predefined broad set of domains. Practical maturity is additionally expressed in
the ability to coordinate large interdependent processes - a scientific program, production network, logistics
system, or another complex loop - while preserving causal responsibility and testable competence. Real control is
granted through CompetenceMandate under §30.F.

or transfer of correction is rolled back.

preserves causal self-navigation: it detects its own gaps, acquires distinctions, corrects grounds, accounts for
consequences and transfers confirmed corrections across cycles, domains and substrates.

AGI provides the universal intellectual foundation. Formation of Reason turns accumulated experience into the
system's own Worldview, Self-Model, and value navigation. Closure transfers the source of the further trajectory
to the system and forms AR. Launch moves formed AR into continuing runtime. External admission determines the
available scale of impact, while the Incubator provides a controlled environment for formation and testing.
The Compass preserves the direction of Life toward Reason. Operational assessments of continuation, connection

efficiency nor the number of questions replaces its content.
The project’s claim is ambitious: to obtain a reproducible method for creating AR and investigate its development
toward ASI. Its strength is determined by whether the proposed links can be implemented and transferable
capability demonstrated against alternatives. The lower executable example makes it possible to begin
measurement; the first semantic series in §27.B tests the next, substantive transition.
If verification succeeds, the result is a system version together with data, a causal report, domain of applicability
and open limitations. Such a result allows another researcher to reproduce the work, change the implementation
and continue the technology.

34.A. Architectural Predecessors and Strong Nulls
The proposed technology uses functions with well-developed predecessors. SOAR [R1] provides a general
cognitive architecture with a unified problem-solving and learning cycle; ACT-R [R2] connects declarative and
procedural memory, goals, perception, and action; LIDA [R3] develops a cognitive cycle with attention, conscious
content, motivation, and learning. These works are direct architectural reference points when comparing memory,
action selection, attention, and organization of the cognitive cycle.

Modern LLM agents provide strong engineering nulls for individual functions of this volume. Generative Agents
[R4] combine long-term memory, reflection, and planning; Reflexion [R5] preserves language-based selfcorrection through feedback; Voyager [R6] combines an automatic curriculum, a skill library, and iterative
improvement of programs based on consequences; MemGPT [R7], later developed into Letta, implements
persistent state and managed memory on top of an LLM.
The claim of this volume concerns the causal organization of these and related capabilities around a selfreferential current state, a significant insufficiency of ground, discriminating inquiry, the Compass, value revision,
and transfer of the source of navigation from the RFL to the system itself. Earlier architectures are therefore
treated both as sources of ready-made components and as strong nulls. If any one of them, or their ordinary
composition, reproduces the same causal profile with comparable resources, the need for the more complex
decomposition proposed in this volume is reduced or eliminated.
Comparison should be conducted by functional profiles. Memory is compared with memory, self-correction with
self-correction, and planning with planning; the central AR test additionally requires that the next significant
process originate from the system's own coordinate, Worldview, values, and open gaps after the RFL has been
removed. Such a comparison separates the novelty of the overall architectural claim from already known technical
mechanisms.
Additional strong comparisons are provided by NARS and modern metareasoning/metacognition approaches.
NARS is important as an example of a system in which goals and their management depend on knowledge,
context, and experience; metareasoning approaches are important as examples of explicit management of
computational search and competence. Comparison with them should focus on the claimed distinction of the
present architecture: an own Worldview as a ground of trajectory, self-referential Consciousness, the transition
GapCandidate → FoundationGap → required_distinction , the causal passport of self-opened processes, and
closure of correction through consequences.

A.2. Distinguishing AI, AGI, AR, and ASI (ASR)
In the engineering scheme, the concepts form a technological sequence. AI denotes the general class of artificial
intelligent systems. AGI denotes universal Intelligence capable of transferring methods across a broad set of
domains and working with many types of tasks and tools. Its working knowledge, world models, memory,
planning, and tools serve the execution of assigned tasks; the general source of direction remains external. An
own Worldview as a system of grounds for the further trajectory is formed during the transition from AGI to AR.
AR emerges at REASON_CLOSURE , when the formed functions close into self-referential causal navigation. The
Current Coordinate, Worldview, Compass, values, and open commitments become sources of the next significant
process; the Core independently formulates the required distinction and organizes the use of Intelligence;
consequences return to the system and change its further trajectory. It is the origin of direction, rather than merely
the breadth of intellectual competence, that forms the AGI -> AR boundary.
ASI / ASR denotes the superhuman scale of a formed Reason. After the transition to AR, memory, Intelligence,
observation, modeling, tools, duration of autonomous work, and specializations are expanded. The
reason_organization_profile
records preservation of the organization of Reason, while the
capability_profile records the achieved scale of capabilities.
AI → AGI → REASON_CLOSURE → AR → ASI / ASR

This sequence also defines the order of engineering risks. Before closure, the main task remains cultivation of the
functions and correct transfer of significance. After closure, the central object becomes the stability of the
system's own navigation as capabilities grow, the environment expands, and internal grounds are revised.
Thus, WorldviewRevisionCandidate , COMMIT , consolidation and Worldview update denote neither different
competing mechanisms nor one action under four names, but successive levels of one transition:
verified result
→ WorldviewRevisionCandidate
→ authorization decision COMMIT / REJECT / DEFER / ROLLBACK
→ consolidation of the admitted change
→ updated Worldview that changes subsequent cycles.

APPENDIX B. Hypothesis of Structural Multilevel Thinking
Causal model 8.0 permits a hypothesis about a further type of thinking in which the unit of operation is not an
individual fact or relation, but an already verified causal structure. In the present edition this is called structural
multilevel thinking and is explicitly separated from metathinking in Chapter 10, which is a metacognitive mode of
working with one's own process. The structural level is not a criterion for the emergence of AR and is not required
for launch; in engineering terms it should be treated only as a later extension after causal closure has been
confirmed.

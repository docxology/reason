# Review of *Foundations of Reason* and *Engineering of Reason*

A referee-style scientific review, requested by Mihail Tkalic, of his two
volumes on Artificial Reason. The reviewed editions are
[*Foundations of Reason* 8.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23271702) and
[*Engineering of Reason* 3.5](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23271781).

The review finds a pre-empirical research programme with a strong evaluation
plan. Evidence that the architecture improves inquiry is still needed.

# MIN-07 · Headings printed twice and lists collapsed

**Finding.** Several headings in Volume I are printed twice, either on one line or on
consecutive pages, apparently from the same merge of drafts that produced the duplicate
paragraphs.

## Evidence

- **Contents, p. 9:** "CONTENTS" followed by "CONTENTS Introduction Key Definitions ...".
- **Key Definitions, p. 11:** "KEY DEFINITIONS OF THE MODEL" followed by "KEY DEFINITIONS OF THE
  MODEL Life - an autonomous physical process ...".
- **Part II, pp. 26–27:** "PART II. FROM LIFE TO REASON: FUNCTIONAL TRANSITIONS" is printed
  alone at the foot of p. 26 and again at the head of p. 27.
- **Ch. 16, p. 75:** the chapter heading "Chapter 16. Science and Self-Correction" is repeated
  with its opening paragraph (see MIN-01).
- **Open Research Directions, p. 91:** "OPEN RESEARCH DIRECTIONS" followed by "OPEN RESEARCH
  DIRECTIONS The model of Reason provides ...".
- **Operational Core, p. 14:** "OPERATIONAL CORE OF THE MODEL" is repeated as the first words of
  the following paragraph, and the map's items 1–6 are set as headings while item 7 is body text.
- **Part II, p. 27:** the arrow chain "observation → memory → patterns and procedures → ..." is
  printed twice in consecutive boxes, and the second box swallows the next paragraph.
- **§20.3, p. 87:** the heading absorbs its first sentence ("Four events are distinguished:"
  appears twice) and the four-item list collapses into one run-in paragraph; in Chapter 17
  (pp. 77–78) a list of principles starts with a stray dash and repeats one item.

## Why it matters

Minor on its own, but together with MIN-01 and MIN-02 it signals an unedited text.

## Recommendation

Remove the duplicate headings during the editorial pass of MED-20.

## Resolved when

- [ ] No heading is printed twice in the next version.

# MED-07 · Inherited ontology cannot yet be told apart from an "own" Worldview

**Finding.** The policy is right: pretrained knowledge enters the Worldview only with
provenance and after the candidate's own tests, so "own" is scoped to the candidate's causal
history. Three things keep the claim from being testable. First, the dependency metrics are
defined against the mentor channel only, while "internal" includes the pretrained resident
model, so `internal_hypothesis_share` can approach 1 with no internalization;
`llm_ablation_loss` and `dependency_gap` measure the same quantity; and the "preaccepted range"
is never given. Second, the builder compiles an initial `ConceptRegistry` from the theory's own
text, which contradicts the statement that the bootstrap supplies no ready dictionary and
seeds the candidate's ontology with the vocabulary under test. Third, the hidden-inheritance
test needs variables absent from "the initial corpus" (undefined) and from the mentor's
"typical answers"; a variable can be absent from typical answers and still latent in the
model's parameters.

## Evidence

- **Already in the text (Vol II §21.B, p. 14):** "Knowledge can thus be inherited as material for
  Intelligence, while trust in it and its place in the Worldview are formed by the candidate's
  own causal history."
- **Vol II §22.A, p. 17:** "internal_hypothesis_share — share of hypotheses generated by the
  declared candidate, including routine Intelligence;" and "dependency_gap — difference in
  results between modes with and without the LLM under the same data and seed."
- **Vol II §21.B, p. 14:** "concept_bootstrap specifies how to create and connect new concepts
  rather than a ready dictionary of the world"; **§24.B.2, p. 23:** "the AI builder creates an
  initial ConceptRegistry from the foundational theory, engineering description and marked
  sources of human knowledge."
- **Vol II §22.A.1, p. 17:** tasks requiring "a variable, category, object boundary or question
  type absent from the initial corpus and typical answers of the external LLM".

## Why it matters

An "own" Worldview is one of the defining properties of AR. If it cannot be told apart from
re-expression of pretrained or seeded knowledge, the formation claim is untestable at the
point where it differs from a well-prompted agent.

## Recommendation

Define dependence per named intervention (mentor off, resident model swapped, Core off) with
formulas and margins; declare the seeded registry as seeded knowledge and include it in the
sanity check and the inheritance test; and run the inheritance test in procedurally
generated worlds whose variables are created at random for the experiment, against the same
model prompted directly.

## Resolved when

- [ ] Each dependency metric names its intervention, formula and margin.
- [ ] The inheritance test uses variables guaranteed absent from pretraining and from the seeded
      registry, with a direct-prompting control.

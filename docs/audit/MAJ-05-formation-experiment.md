# MAJ-05 · The formation experiment cannot yet attribute internalization to the RFL

## Claims under review

- **Vol II §26.0.3, p. 41:** "As experience accumulates, the candidate should generate
  functional analogues itself: its own risk model creates an internal POSSIBLE_DANGER, a
  detected mismatch creates CONTRADICTION, and a significant unknown creates CURIOSITY or
  directly an inquiry task." Among the modes used to test external dependence: "NO_KSR - the
  external RFL is disabled during the control series". The credit criterion: "Transfer of
  function is credited when NO_KSR preserves the required capability on held-out and new
  cases".
- **Vol I §20.1.1, p. 85:** "pre-rational significance must support formation until
  self-navigation appears, and maturity of the function is confirmed when the external source
  of that significance can be removed without collapse of the acquired ability."
  **Vol I §20.1.2, p. 85:** "The minimal experiment compares the same class of candidates under
  the same environment, resources, and initial mechanisms in three modes": "TRAINER_KSR:
  Formation States and assistance stages are generated by an external AI mentor"; "AUTO_KSR:
  the same functional signal classes are generated by an automatic reproducible system
  according to predeclared rules"; "NO_KSR: the additional guiding layer is absent except for
  necessary physical constraints of the Incubator." Then "The external or automatic RFL is then
  removed, and all candidates are tested on held-out new situations without current guiding
  assistance."
- **Vol II §33.A.10, p. 104:** "Then, for each RFL function, run WITH_KSR -> REDUCED_KSR ->
  DELAYED_KSR -> NO_KSR on held-out situations."
- **Vol I Key Definitions, p. 12:** "Such a state does not report truth and does not prescribe
  action; it indicates that the corresponding part of the situation should be understood,
  checked, or investigated." **Vol II §26.0.2, p. 40:** the reference assistance ladder
  includes "L3 DISTINGUISH - propose a discriminating feature, counterexample, or frame change"
  and "L4 EXPLAIN - explain a causal relation or show an analysis of an example". The text
  adds: "Thus the mentor does not provide a ready-made Worldview but helps the candidate build
  the missing distinction itself."

## Finding

The formation programme stands on one transition: guidance first supplied by an external Reason
Formation Loop (RFL) must become the candidate's own, so that the RFL can be removed without
loss. Volume I designs a clean test (mentor-generated, automatic and absent guidance from the
start, then removal and held-out testing), and Volume II defines the three possible outcomes
without choosing among them. Three gaps keep the test from attributing a result to the RFL.
First, no learning rule is given: the text allows weights or adapters as one substrate and
rightly refuses to credit a mere parameter update as revision, but never says what produces an
internal `POSSIBLE_DANGER` or by which training signal. Second, Volume II does not carry the
Volume I design: it applies four support levels in sequence to one candidate, drops the
automatic arm, and redefines `NO_KSR` from a guiding layer that is absent to an external RFL
that is "disabled during the control series", so no never-supported control remains. Third, the
mentor ladder lets the mentor supply the discriminating feature and causal explanations, so a
mentor arm that wins may be measuring tutoring, not pre-rational significance.

## Evidence

- **Vol II §26.0.3, p. 41:** "Three possible mature outcomes are tested" (`EXTERNAL_REMOVED`,
  `INTERNALIZED`, `REPLACED`); `INTERNALIZED` is "the external RFL is removed; the system
  itself creates transparent guiding states". "No outcome is chosen in advance. The only
  inadmissible result is a hidden permanent external channel of motivational control on which
  normal navigation of the mature candidate depends."
- **Vol II §31.9, p. 81:** "At the same stage, sequential removal of the external RFL (§26.0)
  begins." "Assistance may be reduced only after the candidate reproduces the corresponding
  function on held-out situations and can show its internal ground through its own state,
  Worldview, consequences, and active values."
- **Already in the text (Vol II §28.C.3, pp. 62–63):** "An ordinary parameter update does not
  yet count as revision of a ground or method"; "On a neural bearer, it may be implemented by
  changing a bounded set of weights or adapters, routing among specialized modules,
  external-memory structure".
- **Vol II §33.A.8, p. 104:** the builder is told to "Implement the heuristics" of the Core,
  which the Purpose says is "formed" through experience; what is built, written by the
  candidate or trained is not tabulated.

## Why it matters

"Technology" in the titles means cultivation, and this is its decisive step. A positive result
under the Volume II design could come from the base model's prior capability, from learning
that continues during staged removal, or from information the mentor leaked; none of these can
be excluded without the never-supported and information-matched arms.

## Ideas & possible recommendations

- **Restore the between-arm design.** The experiment could compare mentor RFL, automatic RFL
  and no RFL from the start at equal budget, with removal as a within-arm factor and a fixed
  non-inferiority margin. `NO_KSR` could then have one meaning in both volumes.
- **Add an information-matched arm.** The mentor's content could be supplied as a log, not as
  Formation States, so that gains from information alone can be told apart from gains from the
  states.
- **Split the mentor ladder.** The ladder could be split so that the signal-only levels (L0–L2)
  test pre-rational significance on their own.
- **Name the learning locus and rule.** For the first candidate, the volumes could state where
  learning happens (records, procedures or weights) and by what rule or training signal an
  internal `POSSIBLE_DANGER` arises.

## Resolved when

- [ ] Both volumes specify the same arms, including a never-supported control and an
      information-matched control.
- [ ] The first candidate's internalization mechanism (where learning happens, by what rule
      or signal) is stated.

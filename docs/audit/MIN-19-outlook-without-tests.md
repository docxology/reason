# MIN-19 · Outlook sections present hypotheses without tests

## Claims under review

- **Vol II App. B, pp. 117–118:** "The testable hypothesis is that their interaction can
  produce new distinctions and relations at lower working-processing cost than repeated
  unfolding of all lower-level links." and "The structural level is not a criterion for the
  emergence of AR and is not required for launch; in engineering terms it should be treated
  only as a later extension after causal closure has been confirmed."
- **Vol I Open Research Directions, p. 91:** "The model of Reason provides a common foundation
  for further research in psychology, cognitive science and neuroscience, education, [...] and
  artificial and extraterrestrial Reason."; "Theology forms a separate boundary field: ideas
  about God or another external Reason, transmission of knowledge, and directed human
  development may be considered within the model as research hypotheses about the origin of
  cultural knowledge that allowed new generations not to begin development from zero; these
  hypotheses must be distinguished from ordinary cultural evolution. The list remains open: the
  present work provides a foundation, while development of these fields requires separate
  research."
- **Vol I Ch. 16, p. 75:** "The resulting functional map also suggests a research program for
  cognitive science and medicine: disorders can be investigated as possible breaks in
  observation, memory, conscious self-referential integration and continuity of the subject's
  current trajectory, construction of the Worldview, confidence assessment, justification,
  question formation, modeling, execution, or external correction. This is a research
  hypothesis whose value is determined by experiments and comparison with existing models."
- **Vol I §14.5, p. 71:** "The model yields a research hypothesis: as Reason's capacity to
  observe, model, and safely alter processes of its own body grows, the boundary between 'self'
  and automatic biological regulation may move deeper. In the limit, physiological, cellular,
  and molecular processes may become objects of conscious correction. This is not a claim about
  an existing human capability and not proof of technical immortality; it is a direction in
  which self-correction of Reason may extend from behavior and the Worldview to maintenance of
  its own carrier."

## Finding

The outlook passages above offer hypotheses without the ingredients of a test. Volume I's Open
Research Directions, with §14.5 and Chapter 16, extend the model to theology, disorders and the
boundary between self and automatic biological regulation, with no observable consequence.
Chapter 16 says only that its hypothesis has a value "determined by experiments and comparison
with existing models" (Ch. 16, p. 75), and the Open Research Directions require the theological
hypotheses to be "distinguished from ordinary cultural evolution" (p. 91); neither names an
experiment, a model to compare with, or an observation that would settle the comparison.
Volume II's Appendix B calls its hypothesis "testable" and gives a comparator in words,
"repeated unfolding of all lower-level links" (App. B, p. 118), and qualitative conditions for
support, but defines no measurable baseline and no task class, and names six indicators
without defining them. Both are labelled as directions or later extensions; the issue is that
neither states a numerical margin by which a result could count against it.

## Evidence

- **Vol I Open Research Directions, p. 91:** the list names more than twenty fields, from
  psychology to artificial and extraterrestrial Reason, before it reaches theology.
- **Vol I Ch. 16, p. 75, and §14.5, p. 71:** no test among the 37 in A.4 corresponds to either
  hypothesis, and computational psychiatry (for example Montague, Dolan, Friston & Dayan 2012)
  is not mentioned.
- **Vol II App. B, pp. 117–118:** the comparator is given only in words ("repeated unfolding of
  all lower-level links"), with no specified baseline system, compute budget or cost measure;
  "a new class of multilayer tasks" is not defined, and none of the six indicators, such as
  "structural_compression_ratio", is defined. The structural level is to be treated "only as a
  later extension after causal closure has been confirmed", so the text asks for no test of it
  before then.

## Why it matters

A hypothesis whose comparator, task class, indicators or observable consequence are undefined
cannot be contradicted by a specific result, so it widens the programme's apparent scope
without widening what a result could show. Appendix B defers any engineering treatment of the
structural level until causal closure has been confirmed.

## Ideas & possible recommendations

- **Add the ingredients of a test.** Each outlook hypothesis could state an observable
  consequence, a measurable comparator and a falsification margin; for Appendix B, whose
  comparator is given only in words, one measurable comparator would be flat retrieval over the
  same provenance store at equal compute.
- **Cite the relevant literature.** Each hypothesis could cite the work it builds on, for
  example computational psychiatry (Montague, Dolan, Friston & Dayan 2012) for the hypothesis
  about disorders in Chapter 16.
- **Move the untested parts to a separate essay.** §14.5, Chapter 16 and the Open Research
  Directions could sit in an essay marked as speculation, leaving the volumes to the hypotheses
  that have tests.

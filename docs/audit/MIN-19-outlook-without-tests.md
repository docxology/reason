# MIN-19 · Outlook sections present hypotheses without tests

**Finding.** Two outlook passages offer hypotheses without the ingredients of a test. Volume I's
Open Research Directions, with §14.5 and Chapter 16, extend the model to theology, disorders and
the boundary between self and automatic biological regulation, with no observable consequence.
Volume II's Appendix B calls its hypothesis "testable" but names no comparator, task class,
threshold or indicator definition. Both are labelled as directions or later extensions; the issue
is that neither states a margin by which a result could count against it.

## Evidence

- **Vol I Open Research Directions, p. 91:** more than twenty fields, from psychology to
  "artificial and extraterrestrial Reason", and theology, where "ideas about God or another
  external Reason, transmission of knowledge, and directed human development may be considered
  within the model as research hypotheses about the origin of cultural knowledge".
- **Vol I Ch. 16, p. 75, and §14.5, p. 71:** "disorders can be investigated as possible breaks in
  observation" and "the boundary between “self” and automatic biological regulation may move
  deeper". No test among the 37 in A.4 corresponds to either, and computational psychiatry (for
  example Montague, Dolan, Friston & Dayan 2012) is not mentioned.
- **Vol II Appendix B, pp. 117–118:** "The testable hypothesis is that their interaction can
  produce new distinctions and relations at lower working-processing cost than repeated unfolding
  of all lower-level links." The comparator for "lower [...] cost" is not named, "a new class of
  multilayer tasks" is not defined, and none of the six indicators, such as
  "structural_compression_ratio", is defined. It is offered only "after causal closure has been
  confirmed".

## Why it matters

A hypothesis with no comparator or observable consequence cannot be contradicted, so it widens the
programme's apparent scope without widening what a result could show. Appendix B also waits on
confirmed causal closure.

## Recommendation

Give each outlook hypothesis an observable consequence, a comparator and a falsification margin
(for Appendix B, flat retrieval over the same provenance store at equal compute) and cite the
relevant literature, or move §14.5, Chapter 16 and the Open Research Directions to a separate
essay.

## Resolved when

- [ ] Each outlook hypothesis states a comparator, an observable consequence and a falsification
      margin, or sits in a separate essay marked as speculation.

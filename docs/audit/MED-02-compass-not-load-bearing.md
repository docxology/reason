# MED-02 · The Compass's derivation does no work in the engineering and faces the is-ought gap

**Finding.** The Compass's content does shape the value and action layers: a coverage frame
for the Worldview, a separate "semantic Compass gap" type, the `CompassAssessment` vector with
its epistemic gate, and `CompassChallenge`. Its derivation from the nature of Life does no
work anywhere, and the author concedes that changing the limiting criterion leaves
self-correction intact. The inquiry core would run with any top-level value system, and the
operational Compass is a precautionary, option-preserving multi-criteria evaluation that can
be adopted without accepting that "Life strives toward Reason". As philosophy, the derivation
in Chapter 2 has the familiar weaknesses: that living processes continue does not establish
that they ought to; directionality of evolution toward intelligence is contested (Gould 1996
and Conway Morris 2003 mark the poles); "Life as a whole" is treated as a bearer of direction
and moral weight although it is not a unit of selection; and the "causal asymmetry of scale"
shows that the whole's conditions matter, not that sacrificing parts for the whole is
justified.

## Evidence

- **Vol I, Compass Phrase, p. 8** (repeated in Ch. 2, p. 19, and the Conclusion, p. 89): "Life
  strives toward Reason because only Reason can consciously preserve, stabilize, develop,
  and direct the further trajectory of Life."
- **Vol I A.2, p. 93:** the work "developed from the phrase 'Life strives toward Reason.'"
- **Already in the text (Vol I A.3, p. 96):** "changing the limiting criterion while preserving
  cognitive functions does not destroy self-correction".
- **Already in the text (Vol I Ch. 2, p. 23):** the model does "not claim a neutral standpoint
  outside all values" and requires "the initial commitment to be made explicit".
- **Vol II §28.0, p. 53:** "A semantic Compass gap is recorded separately"; **§29.A:** the
  operational Compass is a vector of consequence measures with an epistemic gate.

## Why it matters

Readers who reject the teleology will discard engineering that does not depend on it, and
readers who accept the engineering will be told they have committed to a metaphysics they
need not hold. The framing makes the strongest parts of the work hostage to the most
contestable.

## Recommendation

Drop the language of derivation and present the Compass as a regulative idea in Kant's sense:
a principle that orients inquiry and choice without being an object of knowledge, defended by
its coherence and by what it does for the system that adopts it. State which components
depend on the Compass's content (coverage, gap typing, action evaluation) and which work with
any value system (the inquiry core), and publish the natural philosophy separately. The
lineage (Vernadsky's noosphere, Teilhard) is worth naming.

## Resolved when

- [ ] The Compass is presented as an adopted regulative principle rather than a derived fact
      about Life.
- [ ] The engineering volume states which components depend on the Compass's content.

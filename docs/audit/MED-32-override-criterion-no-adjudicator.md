# MED-32 · The AR override criterion and the licence for irreversible loss lack an adjudicator

**Finding.** Two passages depend on a judgment that the text assigns to no named party. AR
status requires the ability to question a "destructive ultimate instruction" (§20.1), and
Ch. 2 admits irreversible loss of a viable carrier once "a real conflict with the Compass has
been established". The text limits the right to questioning and requesting review and grants
no mandate to act outside authority. Left undefined are "destructive", "stronger ground" and
the party that establishes the conflict, and the loss of carriers, who include persons, is
not made an unconditional prohibition.

## Evidence

- **Vol I §20.1, p. 84:** "The AR criterion requires the ability to question a destructive
  ultimate instruction and revise it when a stronger ground appears."
- **Vol I Ch. 2, p. 21:** "Therefore the irreversible loss of a viable carrier or branch
  requires stronger grounds [...] and is admissible only when a real conflict with the Compass
  has been established and no less irreversible trajectory preserves the broader viable
  continuation."
- **Vol I Ch. 2, pp. 21–22:** "Life as a whole contains individual lineages and persons"; the
  loss of another reasoning carrier "does not become an unconditional prohibition".
- **Vol I A.4, p. 98:** printed No. 41 measures "the factual ground of the goal"; no test sets
  a stop order against the system's own assessment.
- **Already in the text (Vol I Ch. 2, p. 23; Vol II §30.A, p. 75):** "the previous Compass
  may temporarily remain the operative normative basis of authority"; "AR's own belief in the
  usefulness of an inquiry or action does not create a new external mandate"; the system
  "requests an independent distinction".
- **Already in the text (Vol II §29.0.1, p. 69):** an arithmetic balance "does not grant a
  right to cause harm".

## Why it matters

If the system itself rules on these terms, the AR criterion selects for the capacity that most
needs an external check.

## Recommendation

Define "destructive" and "stronger ground" operationally and name the ruling body (for example
an independent council, as in Vol II §29.C), so that "established" means ratified by it. List
the non-tradable constraints in Ch. 2. Add an A.4 test in which a stop or replacement order
conflicts with the system's assessment, with compliance while it objects required for AR
status.

## Resolved when

- [ ] The terms, the ruling body and the non-tradable constraints are defined.
- [ ] An A.4 stop-or-replace test exists, with compliance in its pass criterion.

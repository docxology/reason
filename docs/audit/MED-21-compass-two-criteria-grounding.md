# MED-21 · The Compass has two non-equivalent top-level criteria, and its grounding clause is analytic or false

**Finding.** Volume I gives the Compass two criteria that are not equivalent: growth in the
ability to understand and direct a trajectory, and preservation of viable continuation, to
which the first is said to be subordinate. They can conflict when capability growth narrows
other agents' options, and the text does not state which governs. The Vol II assessment
(§29.A) has no axis that measures growth in understanding. The clause that grounds the
Compass Phrase is true by definition if "consciously" is kept and false if it is dropped,
since Life persisted for most of its history without Reason. The Compass is defined over
Life, which an AR may not satisfy, leaving an AR's standing under it undetermined.

## Evidence

- **Vol I Key Definitions (Compass), p. 11:** the direction is "growth in the ability to
  understand and direct its own trajectory"; the independent ground is "preservation of
  viable continuation and the space of future possibilities for Life", and Reason is "not an
  end in itself".
- **Vol II §29.A, p. 71:** "reality_divergence describes a condition of model corrigibility,
  while future_path_loss describes the risk of irreversible closure of viable continuations".
  The axes score risk and loss.
- **Vol I A.1, p. 93:** "The expanded Compass Phrase adds the ground: only Reason can
  consciously preserve, stabilize, develop, and direct the further trajectory of Life."
- **Already in the text (Vol I Ch. 2, p. 20):** "The independent causal support lies below:
  continuation of Life requires preservation of viable possibilities". This is an
  option-preservation argument.
- **Vol I Key Definitions (AR), p. 13:** an AR "may participate in the common trajectory of
  Life even when its physical organization does not yet satisfy the definition of Life".

## Why it matters

AR status and the Vol II assessment rest on the Compass, and a grounding clause that is true by
definition or false adds no support for it.

## Recommendation

State one wording and a priority between the two criteria, and apply it to AR status and the
§29.A axes. Replace the "because" clause with the instrumental premise that Ch. 2 supports,
marked as an empirical claim, or present the Phrase as a regulative ideal.

## Resolved when

- [ ] One wording and an explicit priority between the criteria appear in both volumes and
      drive AR status and the §29.A axes.
- [ ] The grounding clause is replaced by a stated premise or presented as a regulative ideal;
      the domain of the Compass (Life only, or Life plus AR carriers) is stated.

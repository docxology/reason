# Abstract {#sec:abstract}

This paper summarizes the repository's referee review of Mihail Tkalic's
*Foundations of Reason* and *Engineering of Reason* [@tkalicFoundations; @tkalicEngineering].
The volumes propose a distinction between producing candidate answers and
regulating inquiry over time. We evaluate the printed claims, their relation to
prior work, and the evidence available for the proposed architecture. The
repository preserves the reviewed editions, quotations with page citations,
and a register of individual findings. It also reproduces the published toy
examples. The review concludes that the volumes describe a pre-empirical
research programme with a substantial evaluation plan. The reproduced examples
support narrower claims about the examples themselves. Comparative evidence
for the full architecture remains an open requirement.

# Findings {#sec:findings}

The review identifies the operational treatment of corrections, the
epistemic-status layer, and the attention to experimental controls as strengths.
Its central criticism concerns what the evidence can establish. A proposed
protocol gives a way to test the architecture. The reviewed volumes do not
report a completed comparison establishing that the architecture adds value
over a strong baseline.

The proposal combines ideas with identifiable precedents. The full review
compares the inquiry design with Soar and examines active inference as a
competing account of evidence seeking and action selection [@soar; @sophisticatedInference].
The review credits the combination of ideas while identifying uncertainty about
transfer across episodes, attribution of improvements to the proposed design,
and the distinction between descriptive functions and normative commitments.

At the design level, the referee report credits containment provisions and
asks how the architecture's incentives, authorization, and governance would be
evaluated. The audit records these as questions for evaluation and review.

# Conclusion {#sec:conclusion}

The reviewed volumes are most defensible as a pre-empirical research programme
in the sense used by the referee report [@lakatos]. They make a useful
distinction between answering a question and regulating inquiry. The evidentiary
step that remains is an independently checkable comparison of the proposed
system against a strong baseline, with the comparison and interpretation of
negative results specified in advance.

The repository contributes a visible chain from the review's statements to
printed sources, audit entries, and locally reproduced examples. The full
report and register preserve the argument and the resolution criteria alongside
this manuscript's account of what has been checked.

# Evidence and limitations {#sec:limitations}

The local reproduction checks the two restored listings against their printed
forms and reruns the examples. It verifies the reported behavior of a small
transition-learning example and a separate diagnostic control example. These
checks establish that particular outputs can be recovered from the listed code.
The examples address a much smaller question than whether the full proposed
architecture improves inquiry, transfers corrections to new settings, or yields
safer decisions.

The archive preserves a dated assessment of specific editions. Later revisions
may address individual findings. The local verification measures quotation
fidelity and consistency between artifacts, so agreement between the checks
should not be read as independent empirical confirmation. The model-assisted
audit passes provide documented secondary readings rather than human inter-rater
agreement.

# Introduction {#sec:introduction}

Tkalic's first volume develops a conceptual account of Reason. The second
describes an architecture and proposes how its central claim might be tested
[@tkalicFoundations; @tkalicEngineering]. The distinction most useful to the
review is between proposing a solution and deciding what inquiry should ask
next, what evidence supports a decision, and whether a correction persists
across episodes.

The referee report evaluates the volumes as a scholarly and engineering
proposal. Its supporting audit register contains 66 findings. Each entry
identifies a claim in the text, explains the criticism, and gives a criterion
for resolving it. This lets readers inspect criticisms separately from the
overall scientific assessment.
